Reference 10 ("I'm doing this one myself", 60 s, @agentpoly, studied for format only):
- Dev wants something ("Claude. Step aside. I'm doing this one myself."); the AI answers in one or two flat words
  ("Sure." "Nothing." "It's Python." "The computer is fine.").
- Dev's lines are the comedy: a ladder of three that gets more personal ("Is it the semicolon?" / "Is the computer
  broken?" / "Am I broken?").
- Full-screen screen inserts carry the story, with a clock that jumps (12:40, 1:37, 3:12, 4:58 AM).
- A small win, then the AI's warm last line re-frames everything ("I know. That's why I didn't say anything.").
- 21 lines, ~95 words in 60 s; ~0.5 s inside an exchange, 1.5-2 s between exchanges; one shot per line.
- Small plain white captions (no box), no title card; music almost silent (-43 dB) through the struggle and only
  comes in at the win.

Each script below: Dev asks an AI for something huge; the AI answers flat and literal; the ask shrinks on screen;
one honest line; the AI's care shows; a callback laugh last.
