# Episode 1 (new, comedy): "I Tried to Make Claude Disagree With Me" (script for approval, 18 Oct 2026)

Replaces the code-review episode as the channel's first reel (owner, 18 Oct: one AI per video, funny like the
usage-meter and mushroom references). Cast: **Dev** (male voice) and **Claude** only (Codex not in this one).
Set: Dev's desk at night, monitor glow. About 45 s. Original story (fiction). Parody, not affiliated with Anthropic.
Running gag: an "Absolutely right" counter card that ticks up every time Claude says it.

**Title:** I Tried to Make Claude Disagree With Me 😭
**Alternative titles:** "Claude Agrees With EVERYTHING" · "You're Absolutely Right! (6 Times)"
**Ending question (pinned comment):** "How many times has your AI said "You're absolutely right" today? 👇"

**Voice notes (mishearing risks):**
- "CSS" is voiced as letters ("C S S") and shown as `CSS`.
- Lines 10 and 13 are silent beats (no voice): only the Thinking bubble and room tone, so the pause is the joke.
- Line 11 is two sentences with a full stop between them, so "right." and "You're wrong." don't run together.
- Every "You're absolutely right!" is its own take so the delivery can get more cheerful each time.
