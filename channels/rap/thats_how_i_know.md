## Checklist
- One idea, never named ("love" is never sung). Turn: never said it -> bike fixed by dawn (line 3, ~8 s in).
- Syllables per line: 8, 8, 8, 9, 9, 8. 45 words before the repeat.
- Held ends: time (long I), phone / know / cold (OH), dawn / gone (AW): all open. Unrhymed "time" leaves the
  ear waiting; dawn/gone and phone/cold/know build the chain; "know" resolves on the title.
- Stress: "you NEVer SAID it, NOT one TIME"; "my BROken BIKE was FIXED by DAWN"; "THAT'S how you SAID it".
- Misheard risk: "coat" and "cold" share a line; Whisper-check every take.
- Owner's line: slot open (a real detail from the owner's own father or family) to replace or follow line 5.
