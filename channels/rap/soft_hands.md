# Soft Hands (draft 1, awaiting owner approval)

Song 3. Written to the bar of the owner's lyric references (ref 2/4 "She said..." song, ref 3, ref 1):
one abstract idea turned into a person (here: Comfort, the thing that quietly kills dreams), told in pictures,
few words, a twist in the first two lines, an eerie callback at the end.

## Paste into Gemini as ONE message
Create a song WITH VOCALS. A soft, low male voice sings these exact lyrics, clearly audible, every section.
Style: dark melodic rap / R&B storytelling, 70 BPM, slow and spacious, minor key, sparse dark piano, deep sub bass,
soft trap hi-hats, lots of silence between lines. Voice: intimate, close-mic, half-spoken half-sung like someone
telling a secret; the chorus is sung with a simple haunting melody.

## Why it's built this way
- One conceit, never named: "he" is Comfort. The listener works it out in the chorus (key, tea, "rest, it's only
  a week") and the reveal is "wake up at thirty, wondering where you've been".
- Twist in the first two lines (knife -> blanket), like "you think the devil has horns? so did I".
- Every line is a picture: key, tea, dimmed lamp, dust on the strings, father's eyes in the mirror, bare feet.
- Few words, room to breathe: about 2-3 words a second, like refs 2-4. A dangling word on its own line
  ("tomorrow.") is the hesitation itself.
- The final chorus flips ("but not this time ... the rest of it is mine") and the outro undercuts it like a
  thriller: he still has a key. That's the comment-section question: did he really leave?
- Intro + Chorus alone is a complete ~20 s Short (same length and shape as the references).
