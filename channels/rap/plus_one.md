## The lines that carry it
- "never in the photos, still in every frame": she's invisible but always there (photo frame / frame of mind).
- "Y'all get the highlight reel, she gets every take": film take = every attempt at a fake smile.
- "laughing at a joke nobody told on the phone": the fake phone call, shown not told.
- Turn in verse 2: "I keep the door locked tight, then I blame her that it's closed": the singer is the one
  keeping people out. Resolution: one real "come over" and she disappears.
- Final chorus flips "nobody stays" -> "now somebody stays", "she's the only one awake" -> "I'm not the only one".
- Short cut: Intro + Chorus (~25 s), a complete riddle; the comment question is "who is she?".
- Misheard watch: "frame", "take/make", "disappeared".
