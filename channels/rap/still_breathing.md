# Still Breathing (draft 1, awaiting owner approval)

Rap music video channel. Original lyrics; the owner generates the audio in Gemini (or another music app).
No real artist's voice, name or lyrics are imitated.

## Music app prompt
Emotional dark hip-hop, 88 BPM, minor key, half-time trap groove. Sparse sad piano, deep 808 bass, crisp
hi-hats with small rolls, soft vinyl crackle. Gritty, controlled male rap vocal, close-mic, building
intensity; the hook is half-sung, half-rapped and anthemic. Mood: struggling but refusing to quit.

## Structure (88 BPM, 1 bar = 2.7 s)
Intro 2 bars | Hook 4 | Verse 1 8 | Hook 4 | Verse 2 8 | Bridge 2 | Hook 4 | Outro 1  (about 90 s)
Short cut: Verse 1 + Hook, about 33 s. Hook alone also works as a 12-15 s Short.
