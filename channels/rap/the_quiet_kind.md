# The Quiet Kind (draft 1, awaiting owner approval)

Song 2 of the rap channel. Original lyrics. The lyric writing and voice follow refs 2 and 3; the visuals and
emotional arc follow ref 1. The owner makes the audio in Gemini.

## Music app prompt
Soft emotional melodic rap, 68 BPM, slow half-time beat, minor key. Sparse felt piano, soft clean guitar, warm
sub bass, gentle lo-fi drums that leave lots of space, light rain ambience. Low, soft male baritone vocal,
close-mic and breathy, half-sung and half-rapped, tired but warm; the hook is fully sung with a simple melody.
Intro and outro are spoken quietly. Mood: smiling on the outside, breaking on the inside, hopeful at the end.

## How it's built
- Rhyme: each section runs ONE vowel sound (refs 2/3): hook = long A (break / days / name / complain / fake),
  verse 1 = long I (hi / why / time / night / alright / fine / fire / cry), verse 2 = long O (phone / alone /
  home / own / know / slow / show / know).
- Opens on a quote with a twist ("Thanks." ... "I practiced."). "I'm fine" repeats with a flip in the final hook
  ("one day I'll say it and it won't be fake"). The "If I could ..., I wouldn't ..., I'd just ..." wish in verse 2.
- Ends on the smallest real moment: someone finally asks, and the answer is honest.
- About 2:30 at 68 BPM (1 bar = 3.5 s). Short cut: Intro + Hook + Verse 1 (~45 s), or Intro + Hook alone (~20 s).
