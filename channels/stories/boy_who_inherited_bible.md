# STORY BIBLE — THE BOY WHO INHERITED ANOTHER BODY (the owner's own story, pasted 27 Oct 2026, verbatim)

Core Premise

A psychological thriller about death, consciousness transfer, wealth, identity, revenge, and survival.

Main Character: Neil

- Age: 17.
- A talented, analytical trader.
- Begins with $1,000 and grows his trading account to $5,014,382.61.
- His intelligence and trading skills are central to his identity.
- Dies while protecting Maya.
- Instead of disappearing after death, his consciousness transfers into another living human body according to a mysterious cosmic law.

Maya

- A person deeply important to Neil.
- Neil dies while protecting her.
- His death and their relationship must carry emotional weight.
- Preserve her importance to the original plot.

The Body-Transfer Rule

When Neil dies, his consciousness transfers according to a cosmic law into the nearest eligible living human body.

The established example:

- The nearest eligible person is approximately 100 metres away.
- Neil awakens in the body of Arthur Vance, an 82-year-old billionaire.

Do not casually change this mechanism. Treat the transfer rule as a central mystery whose full explanation should be revealed only when supported by the plot.

Arthur Vance

- Age: 82.
- A billionaire whose body Neil inherits.
- His body is physically old and his health is failing.
- His wealth, social position, family, and enemies become Neil's new reality.
- Neil inherits Arthur's circumstances, not necessarily Arthur's memories, personality, or abilities. Establish any access to memories only through explicit story development.

Established Plot Points

1. Neil is a talented trader who builds his account from $1,000 to $5,014,382.61.
2. His life is connected to Maya, whom he ultimately protects at the cost of his own life.
3. Neil dies.
4. His consciousness transfers to Arthur Vance, an 82-year-old billionaire approximately 100 metres away.
5. Neil wakes in Arthur's failing body and realizes he has survived death in a form he never chose.
6. He must navigate Arthur's wealth, family succession conflict, dangerous enemies, and complicated social position.
7. Neil faces the extraordinary possibility of attending his own funeral while keeping his new identity secret.
8. The story explores whether a person remains the same person when their body, identity, and place in society are taken away.

Important Themes

- Identity versus physical appearance.
- The cost of survival.
- Wealth and power.
- Family succession and betrayal.
- Love, sacrifice, and grief.
- The difference between being alive and truly living.
- Whether intelligence can overcome physical weakness and social danger.

Writing Rules

1. Preserve the established names, ages, amounts, relationships, and plot points.
2. Do not merge this story with DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM or its characters.
3. Do not replace Neil, Maya, or Arthur Vance without explicit permission.
4. Do not change the trading account amount: $5,014,382.61.
5. Do not change Arthur's age: 82.
6. Keep the approximate 100-metre distance relevant to the body-transfer event.
7. Do not invent a definitive explanation for the cosmic law before it is properly developed.
8. Make financial, trading, legal, medical, and family-succession details internally consistent.
9. Build suspense through clues, consequences, character motives, and logical reveals.
10. Avoid arbitrary twists, convenient coincidences, unexplained powers, and plot armour.
11. Give Neil realistic limitations. His trading intelligence does not automatically make him an expert in every field or give his elderly body youthful physical abilities.
12. Maintain continuity across every chapter.
