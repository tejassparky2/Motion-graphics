# DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM, Part 1: "12:17" (script for approval)

Owner's own story (story bible, 27 Oct 2026). This part covers Chapter 1 beats 1-10 in canon order: the classroom at
12:16, the cast, the meteor and the eye, waking underground, the screen, EVENT 00, Momo. Beats 11-12 (THE GLASS
AUCTION and its countdown) open Part 2, so the game's rules get their own episode. Nothing here adds a rule, a
backstory or a reveal the bible doesn't have.

**Built for hook and engagement:**
- **Cold open from the future.** Line 1 tells you the meteor hits at 12:17. Then it jumps back one minute, so every
  ordinary classroom moment is tense: the clock on screen ticks 12:16:00 → 12:17:00 the whole time.
- **Name cards.** Anime freeze-frames: each character's name and one word the first time they appear. That way
  viewers can name them in the comments.
- **One unexplained thing.** The eye in the meteor, kept a mystery.
- **The ending** (changed 27 Oct 2026, owner: "it will just go as story part 1 2 3 so on leave every part with s strong
  hook"): no question to the viewer. Momo announces twenty games, the first an auction for the exit key, and Ren ends it:
  "Twenty of us. One key." Card: PART 2 · THE GLASS AUCTION.
- **Length.** About 60 s, ~165 words.

**Look:** 2D anime style. Japanese school uniforms (gakuran and sailor), Class 3-B sign, noon sun, then a cold grey
concrete chamber.

**Voices:**
- Ren narrates (a calm, low teenage voice, inner monologue).
- Each named student has their own voice.
- Momo: bright, sing-song, with a bell jingle when it moves.
