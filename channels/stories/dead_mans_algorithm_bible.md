# DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM — STORY BIBLE (the owner's own story, pasted 27 Oct 2026, verbatim)

1. Core Premise

- Genre: Psychological thriller, survival games, mystery, apocalypse.
- Setting: Japanese high school and a mysterious underground facility.
- Main event: At exactly 12:17 PM, a meteor strikes. Twenty students from Class 3-B awaken inside an unknown underground facility.
- The facility is connected to a mysterious survival system called DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM.
- Their teacher is missing. Their phones have no signal. Cameras monitor the facility.
- The status of Earth is unknown.
- The students must survive a sequence of 20 survival games.

2. Main Characters

- Kuroda Ren: Main protagonist. An apparently average, observant, analytical student who thinks strategically.
- Tachibana Yui: Kind, observant, emotionally close to Ren.
- Sakurai Haruto: Ren's witty, mischievous friend. Uses humor to hide tension.
- Kanzaki Riku: Aggressive, domineering student who tries to control others.
- Hayashi Kota: Quiet, intelligent student who is bullied and develops survival plans.
- Shibata Gō: Strong, quiet, loyal student who stands up to bullies.
- Fujimoto Aoi: Analytical, calm, observant student who notices structural and logical details.
- Aoyama Mio: Stylish, socially perceptive student who advises others not to get involved.
- Momo: Small, white, rodent-like mascot with round ears, black eyes, a bow tie, and a bell. It is the administrator of DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM. Its cheerful personality contrasts with the deadly games.

There are exactly 20 students from Class 3-B. Maintain a consistent roster and character identities.

3. Chapter 1 — 12:17

Follow this established sequence:

1. Introduce ordinary classroom life at 12:16 PM.
2. Introduce Ren, Haruto, Yui, and their relationships.
3. Show Riku bullying Kota and Gō intervening.
4. Introduce Aoi and Mio through their reactions.
5. Students notice a rapidly approaching meteor.
6. At 12:17 PM, the meteor strikes. Ren briefly sees a mysterious shape or pattern inside it, resembling an eye.
7. Ren awakens in an underground chamber with all 20 students. The teacher is missing.
8. The students discover cameras and a screen displaying "CLASS 3-B — 20 STUDENTS — 20 SURVIVORS."
9. A system message reads "EVENT 00: IMPACT — STATUS OF EARTH: UNKNOWN."
10. Momo appears and introduces itself as the administrator of DEAD MAN'S ALGORITHM.
11. The first game, THE GLASS AUCTION, begins.
12. End the opening chapter with the students facing the first game and its countdown.

4. Survival Game Rules — The Glass Auction

- Players: 20.
- Starting tokens: 100 per player.
- Time limit: 10 minutes.
- Objective: Obtain the exit key.
- Rule 1: The highest unique bid wins the key.
- Rule 2: All spent tokens are permanently lost.
- Rule 3: If multiple players submit the same highest bid, every player tied at that amount is eliminated.
- Rule 4: If the highest bid is tied, the next-highest unique bid wins.
- Rule 5: If everyone leaves all tokens unspent when time expires, everyone is executed.

These rules are fixed. Do not change, contradict, or add hidden rules.

5. Essential Story Rules

1. Fair-play mystery: Every solution, twist, betrayal, and revelation must be supported by clues planted beforehand.
2. No arbitrary hidden rules: Never introduce a new rule retroactively to force a plot outcome.
3. Logical survival games: Every game must have internally consistent rules, understandable stakes, and a solution readers can reason through.
4. Strategic protagonist: Ren should survive through observation, deduction, planning, and calculated decisions—not unexplained luck.
5. Meaningful character development: Characters must have distinct motives, fears, personalities, alliances, and believable reactions.
6. Consequences matter: Decisions must affect trust, relationships, resources, and survival.
7. Controlled reveals: Mysteries should be answered gradually. Each answer may reveal a deeper question, but earlier clues must remain meaningful.
8. Continuity: Maintain consistent character names, the 20-student roster, chronology, game rules, injuries, relationships, and established facts.
9. No plot armor: Important characters are not automatically safe. Survival and death must follow the established circumstances and rules.
10. Psychological realism: Fear, suspicion, grief, selfishness, cooperation, and betrayal must develop naturally.
11. Momo's characterization: Keep its cheerful, unsettling administrator persona consistent. Do not reveal its true nature without a properly foreshadowed payoff.
12. Meteor mystery: The object Ren sees inside the meteor must remain a meaningful unresolved mystery until sufficient clues justify its explanation.

6. Writing Style

- Write as a cinematic manga with clear panel descriptions, dialogue, narration, expressions, and sound effects.
- Use suspense, visual storytelling, strategic dialogue, and carefully paced reveals.
- Avoid excessive exposition, repetitive dialogue, convenient coincidences, and unexplained abilities.
- Keep the atmosphere tense and unsettling while allowing natural humor and emotional relationships.
- Make every scene serve character development, world-building, mystery, or plot progression.

7. Instructions for Continuing the Story

Treat this document as the authoritative story bible. Preserve all established facts and rules. Before writing each new game or major twist, verify that its mechanics are consistent, its solution is logically discoverable, and its clues appear before the reveal.

Do not rewrite established canon without explicit permission. Do not invent additional backstory or major plot revelations without ensuring they fit the existing continuity.
