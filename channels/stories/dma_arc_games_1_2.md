## Game 2: THE CHARITY (fixed once approved)

- Players: 20. Time limit: 10 minutes.
- Rule 1: Every player keeps the tokens they have.
- Rule 2: A player may give any number of tokens to another player. Gifts are final.
- Rule 3: Tokens cannot be taken, destroyed or spent.
- Rule 4: When time runs out, the player with the fewest tokens is executed.
- Rule 5: If several players tie for the fewest, every one of them is executed.

Why it's clever (and fair): after Game 1 the class holds 1,969 tokens (18 x 100, Kota 99, Riku 70). Twenty equal piles
are impossible (and a 20-way tie would kill everyone), so **someone is always the lowest**. A gift only moves the bottom:
whoever saves the lowest player drops below them. Splitting the gift between many people just creates a tie at the
bottom, and rule 5 kills every one of them. Doing nothing kills exactly one person: Riku, whose greed cost him 30 tokens.
