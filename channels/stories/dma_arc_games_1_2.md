## The rule for every game from now on (owner's direction)

1. Every game has a **loophole in its own rules that lets all players survive**. It is fair play: everything needed is
   in the rules as Momo reads them, and the clue is on screen before Ren explains it (bible rules 1-3).
2. **Ren finds it** and tries to save everyone.
3. **People break it**: greed, fear, betrayal, panic, or someone's sacrifice. That is why most games still kill.
4. Only a few games end with everyone alive. Game 1 is one of them; from Game 2 on, at least one dies in most games.
5. The death always follows from the rules plus a human choice the viewer saw coming (bible rule 9: no plot armour).

Everything below that is not in the bible is **my proposal** and becomes canon only when the owner approves it.

## Game 1: how it ends (Part 3, "The Bids")

**The loophole Ren finds:** the five rules can only kill in two ways: a tie for the highest bid (rule 3) or nobody
spending anything (rule 5). So the class needs **exactly one bid, as small as possible**: eighteen bid zero, one person
bids **one token**. One bid can't tie with anything, so it wins; rule 3 can't fire; somebody spent a token, so rule 5
can't fire. The class loses one token in total and **all 20 live**. Ren also reads the word *permanently* in rule 2:
spent tokens never come back, so whatever they spend here follows them into every later game. That's why the bid must
be tiny.

**What people do to it:** Kota is chosen to bid one ("Nobody in this room is afraid of Kota"). Riku agrees too fast,
then secretly bids **thirty** to win the key himself. Thirty is the highest unique bid, so he wins. There is still no
tie, so **20/20 survive**, but Riku has spent thirty tokens for good and now has the fewest (70). The key opens the
sealed door for everyone; behind it is Game 2.

Answers this needs (proposed): losers aren't punished (Q1); the key opens the door for everyone (Q2); you may bid 0
while others bid (Q4); bids are secret and final (Q5); tokens carry over (Q7). Q3 and Q6 stay open.

## Game 2: THE CHARITY (rules fixed once approved)

A white room. A glass **charity box** in the middle. A screen that shows one number: **PLAYERS: 20**.
Ten minutes. Tokens from Game 1: eighteen students have 100, Kota 99, Riku 70.

- Rule 1: Nobody can take your tokens.
- Rule 2: You may give tokens to anyone, or drop them in the box. Gifts are final.
- Rule 3: The box locks when one minute is left.
- Rule 4: When time runs out, the player with the fewest tokens is executed. If several tie for the fewest, all of them
  are.
- Rule 5: Anyone holding zero tokens is not a player.

Momo, after the rules: "Tokens are precious! You'll want them later!"

**The obvious reading:** someone is always the poorest, so someone always dies, and right now that's Riku.

**The loophole Ren finds (rules 4 + 5):** rule 4 only executes a *player*, and rule 5 says zero tokens means you are
not a player. If all twenty drop **everything** in the box before it locks, there are **no players, so there is no
poorest player. All 20 live.** The price: everyone walks into Game 3 with nothing ("You'll want them later").

**What people do to it (greed, then betrayal of kindness):**
- The class drops everything. Haruto goes first ("Tokens or our lives? That's easy"). The quiet girl, **Nishida Saki
  (seat 14)**, drops hers with a small smile ("I never wanted them"). Riku watches her.
- Riku refuses: "I'm not walking into game three with nothing." Ren warns him: alone, he is the only player, and the
  only player is always the poorest. Momo confirms it. Riku just smiles.
- The box locks. **PLAYERS: 1.** Everyone thinks Riku has killed himself out of greed.
- With ten seconds left, Riku walks to Saki and **gives her one token**. Gifts are final, and the box is locked, so
  she can't drop it. **PLAYERS: 2.** One token makes her a player again, and the poorest one.
- She can give it back to Riku (then he is the only player and dies) or to anyone else (then they die). Ren shouts
  "Give it back to him!" (he is telling her to kill Riku, and he knows it). Saki looks at Riku. She can't do it, and
  Riku chose her **because** he knew she couldn't.
- Time's up: Riku 69, Saki 1. Saki is executed (lights out; when they come back she's gone and her token lies on the
  floor; no gore). **19 survivors.** Riku walks into Game 3 as the only one with anything to spend.

**The clever part, and the clue:** the box locks, but **gifts never do**. Rule 2 lets you give tokens "to anyone"
with no time limit, and rule 3 only locks the box. A viewer who notices that can see Riku's move coming. After the
lock, someone must die (tokens can't be destroyed or taken, so at least one player stays, and the poorest dies); Riku
made sure it wasn't him. Ren's line at the end: "I found the loophole. Riku found the loophole in my loophole."
