# Hindi trial, batch 1: three scripts in Meanwhile Mayank's winning format (draft for owner approval, 29 Oct 2026)

Format copied from what worked for him (research_notes/meanwhilemayank_deep_dive_2026-10-29.md), with our own
topics and words.

**Format:**
- Hinglish narration, one calm male narrator, ~190 words a minute, 2:00-2:20 long. His hits ran 1:32-2:57.
- English on-screen headlines.

**Built like his hits:**
- **Frame 0:** one subject, alone, on creased poster paper.
- **Headline:** a small dark setup line plus ONE big red word, fully on screen by about 2 s.
- **Line 1:** "Socho…" or a question to *you*, with the contradiction inside it.
- **Then:**
  - a reality stamp ("ye koi movie nahi")
  - the name
  - one fact of scale with an Indian stake
  - the mechanism, one step per sentence
- **Re-hook:** one question at about 60%.
- **Ending:** a myth-bust plus what to do, then a "you too?" comment question.

**Look:** real-photo cut-outs (Wikimedia Commons / CC0, or generated locally) on paper texture. Something changes
every ~1-1.5 s, densest in seconds 3-15.

**Topics in his winning lanes:**
- a horror of nature that is also inside you (Toxoplasma)
- an Indian life-or-death stake with practical steps (krait / snakebite, his rabies shape)
- a hidden thing on *your* body (face mites)

The narration below is Roman Hinglish so it's easy to edit. It goes to the voice in Devanagari, as in his captions.
Change any line you like.

**Sources:**
- Berdoy, Webster & Macdonald 2000, *Proc. R. Soc. B*, "Fatal attraction in rats infected with *Toxoplasma gondii*"
  ([PMC1690701](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1690701))
- CDC toxoplasmosis prevention pages (pregnancy, litter box, indoor cats)
- The "1 in 3 worldwide" figure is a widely cited estimate, so it is said as "andaza".

## 2. "Saanp jo sote hue kaatta hai" (common krait, snakebite in India)
**Frame 0:** one black-and-white banded krait on paper. Headline: `IT BITES WHILE YOU` / **`SLEEP`** (red, by 1.8 s).

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Socho, ek saanp jo raat ko, zameen par sote hue insaan ko kaatta hai... aur kaatne par dard bhi nahi hota. | the krait slides across a floor mat; "SLEEP" lands in red |
| 2 | Ye koi kahani nahi hai. Ise kehte hain common krait. Kai jagah log ise karait bolte hain. | specimen label "COMMON KRAIT · FIG. 1" |
| 3 | Bharat mein har saal lagbhag 58,000 log saanp ke kaatne se marte hain. Duniya mein sabse zyada. | flip counter "58,000 / YEAR"; India map dot |
| 4 | Aur sabse dukh ki baat? Iska ilaaj, anti-venom, Bharat mein hi banta hai. | anti-venom vial, red tick, "MADE IN INDIA" |
| 5 | Bharat mein zyadatar maut chaar saanpon se hoti hai: cobra, krait, Russell's viper aur saw-scaled viper. Inhe Big Four kehte hain. | 4 specimen cards slide in one by one |
| 6 | Krait ka zeher nerves par hamla karta hai. Ye nerve aur muscle ke beech ka signal rok deta hai. | nerve → muscle diagram, the signal blocked |
| 7 | Pehle palkein bhaari hoti hain. Phir nigalna mushkil hota hai. Aur phir saans lene wali muscles kaam karna band kar deti hain. | 3-step strip: eyelid, throat, lungs, each turning red |
| 8 | Aur iska kaata itna chhota hota hai ki daant ke nishaan bhi mushkil se dikhte hain. Kai log ise pet dard ya thakaan samajh lete hain. | magnified skin, two faint dots, circle drawn round them |
| 9 | Krait raat ko shikaar karta hai. Garmi aur baarish ke mausam mein ye gharon mein ghus aata hai. | night house cut-away, monsoon rain |
| 10 | Toh agar anti-venom maujood hai... toh itne log marte kyun hain? | **re-hook**; "WHY?" in red |
| 11 | Kyunki zyadatar maut ghar par hi hoti hai. Log hospital time par pahunchte hi nahi. | house icon, hospital far away, a clock ticking |
| 12 | Kai log pehle jhaad-phoonk ke liye jaate hain. Kuch log zakhm kaat ke zeher choosne ki koshish karte hain, ya rassi se kas ke baandh dete hain. | three myth cards, each struck through in red |
| 13 | Ye sab galat hai, aur ilaaj mein der karta hai. | "MYTH" stamp |
| 14 | Agar saanp kaate: ghabraaiye mat. Jis haath ya pair par kaata hai, use hilaaiye mat. | calm figure, limb resting straight |
| 15 | Ring, ghadi aur tight kapde nikaal dijiye. Aur turant us hospital jaaiye jahan anti-venom ho. 108 par call kar sakte hain. | checklist ticks, "108" card |
| 16 | Aur agar zameen par sote ho, toh machhardaani laga ke soiye. Nepal ki ek study mein machhardaani ke saath sone walon ko saanp ke kaatne kaafi kam hue. | mosquito net over a floor mat, krait outside it |
| 17 | Kya aapke ghar ya gaon ke paas kabhi saanp nikla hai? Comment mein bataiye. | `NEAR YOUR` / **`HOME?`**, phone comments card |

**Sources:**
- Suraweera et al. 2020, *eLife*, "Trends in snakebite deaths in India from 2000 to 2019" (~1.2M deaths, ~58,000 a
  year, most at home in rural areas) ([PubMed 32633232](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32633232/))
- WHO snakebite first-aid guidance (no cutting, no suction, no tight tourniquet; immobilise; hospital)
- Chappuis et al. 2007, *Toxicon* (bed nets and snakebite, Nepal)
- "Duniya mein sabse zyada" rests on India carrying about half of global snakebite deaths in the same literature.

## 3. "Aapke chehre par abhi jaanwar hain" (Demodex face mites)
**Frame 0:** one magnified mite on paper. Headline: `ON YOUR FACE` / **`RIGHT NOW`** (red, by 1.8 s).

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Abhi, isi waqt, aapke chehre par chhote-chhote jaanwar rehte hain. Aath pair waale. Aur ye raat ko bahar nikalte hain. | the mite's legs twitch; "RIGHT NOW" lands in red |
| 2 | Ye koi horror movie nahi hai. Inka naam hai Demodex mites. | specimen label "DEMODEX · FIG. 1" |
| 3 | Ek mite lagbhag ek-tihaai millimetre ka hota hai. Aankh se dikhta hi nahi. | ruler, mite next to the 0.3 mm mark |
| 4 | Aur ye kuch logon par nahi... lagbhag har adult par hote hain. Ek study mein 18 saal se upar ke har insaan ke chehre par inka DNA mila. | grid of faces, every one gets a red dot; "100%" |

| 7 | Din mein ye andar chhupe rehte hain. Raat ko jab aap sote ho, ye bahar aate hain, skin par chalte hain, aur mating ke liye doosre mite dhoondte hain. | day/night split: sun → moon, the mites climb out |

| 12 | Aur ek purani kahani thi ki inke potty karne ka raasta hi nahi hota, isliye marte waqt ye saari gandagi chehre par chhod dete hain. | "OLD STORY" card |

| 15 | Inhe poori tarah hataana na mumkin hai, na zaroori. Chehre ko zor se ragadna bekaar hai. Agar palkon mein lagaataar khujli ya jalan ho, toh aankh ke doctor ko dikhaiye. | crossed-out scrub brush; doctor card |
| 16 | Toh aaj raat sone jaao, toh yaad rakhna... aap akele nahi ho. Kya aapko ye pehle se pata tha? Comment mein bataiye. | `YOU ARE NOT` / **`ALONE`**, phone comments card |

**Sources:**
- Thoms et al. 2014, *PLoS ONE* (Demodex DNA on all adults over 18 sampled)
- Smith et al. 2022, *Molecular Biology and Evolution*, and the
  [University of Reading summary](https://reading.ac.uk/news/2022/Research-News/Secret-lives-of-skin-mites-in-our-faces)
  (night mating, melatonin from human skin, gene loss, the anus finding)
