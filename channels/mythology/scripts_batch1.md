# Mythology video 1: Kakudmi, the king who waited a few moments (draft for approval)

**The story in one line:** King Kakudmi takes his daughter Revati to Brahma to ask who she should marry. He waits a
few moments while Gandharvas sing. Brahma tells him 27 chatur-yugas have passed on Earth (over 11 crore years),
everyone he knew is gone, and he should give Revati to Balarama. Back on Earth people have become smaller, his city
Kushasthali is now Dwaraka, and Balarama shortens the too-tall Revati with the tip of his plough.

**Why this one:** little-known, sounds impossible, and it is in the texts. Kakudmi waits a moment, and 11 crore
years pass on Earth.

## Facts it rests on (checked 6 Nov 2026)
- Bhagavata Purana 9.3.29-36: Kakudmi takes Revati to Brahmaloka to ask about a husband. Brahma is hearing the
  Gandharvas' music, so the king waits until it ends. Brahma laughs and says the men he had chosen are long gone;
  "twenty-seven catur-yugas have already passed", and their names are not even heard any more. He tells him to give
  Revati to Baladeva (Balarama), who is on Earth now. The king does, then leaves for Badarikashrama.
- Vishnu Purana 4.1 (Wilson 1840; M. N. Dutt 1894):
  - the singers are the Gandharvas Haha and Huha;
  - the ages that passed "seemed to him as a moment";
  - Brahma says "many ages" (no number), and that Kushasthali "is now called Dwaraka";
  - the king finds mankind "greatly reduced in size and vigour and weakened in intellect";
  - Balarama, seeing "that damsel of excessive height", "shortened her with the end of his ploughshare", and she
    "became like other women".
- Arithmetic: 1 chatur-yuga = 43,20,000 years (the standard Puranic reckoning). 27 of them = 11,66,40,000 years,
  "more than 11 crore".
- Where the texts differ: only the Bhagavata gives the number 27; the plough-and-height detail is from the Vishnu
  Purana. The script names the text at both points.
