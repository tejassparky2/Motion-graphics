# Video 4 for approval: King Bali and Vamana (Diwali / Bali Padyami)

**Style:** like your reference reel. Painted characters (Bali, Vamana, Shukracharya, the giant Trivikrama step) on
painted stage sets, the cream + red headlines, a top label row with "FIG. n", a torn "BALI PADYAMI" tag, torn paper
notes, a checklist and counters. Characters and sets made on the laptop, checked by eye for correct iconography.

**Why this story:** Diwali is tomorrow, and Bali Padyami is the next day (Karnataka; Bali Pratipada elsewhere). Most
people know "three steps"; almost nobody knows the ending: God promised the defeated king he would one day be
king of heaven, and that the giant step pierced the shell of the universe and let the Ganga in.

**Facts:** Bhagavata Purana 8.19.16 (three paces by his own steps), 8.19.32-35 (Shukracharya's warning), 8.20.33-34
(earth, then sky), 5.17.1 (the big toe's nail pierced the covering; the Ganges entered), 8.22.2 (third step on my
head), 8.22.31 (king of heaven in Savarni's time), 8.22.33 (Sutala, "desired even by the demigods"), 8.22.35 ("I
shall always be with you and give you protection"). Bali's yearly return on Bali Pratipada is a belief, and the
script says so. Left out on purpose: "Vishnu became Bali's doorkeeper", which is not in these verses.
