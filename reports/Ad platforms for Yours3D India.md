# Put most of your ad money on Meta

**Meta (Instagram and Facebook) Click-to-WhatsApp ads will help Yours3D India the most.** Google Search comes a distant second. ChatGPT ads come last for now. Meta wins because it matches how you already sell. People see a Reel, tap one button, and land in your WhatsApp chat, where your free AI preview does the selling. Indian agencies report Meta's cost per WhatsApp chat at roughly **₹15 to ₹60**. Meta's learning phase needs about 50 results a week, and a small budget can reach that when you pay for chats. Google Search is useful because it reaches people who are already searching for a gift. But no one has published search volumes or costs for figurine keywords in India, and the main ad click must go to your website, not to WhatsApp. ChatGPT ads are real: **ads went live in India on 27 August 2026**, and Indian businesses could buy them themselves from **4 September 2026**, from **₹725 a day**. But the product is about two months old in India. It needs a website landing page and tracks clicks poorly, and there is only one public Indian cost figure. A sensible start is about **₹1,500 a day: two-thirds on Meta, one-third on Google Search, nothing on ChatGPT yet**. Judge every rupee by **cost per order**, not cost per chat. One warning before you read on. No source anywhere gives ad results for a custom-figurine seller, in India or abroad, so every cost-per-order number below is an estimate you must replace with your own data. Reddit was blocked for the research tools, so no Reddit threads were read directly.

## Meta wins because your buyers already order on WhatsApp

**Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads fit your business almost exactly.** Indian competitors work the same way. My Miniature runs the whole order, with photos, previews and approvals, on WhatsApp, and offers a free 3D preview ([My Miniature](https://myminiature.in/)). Huminity takes WhatsApp orders too, and charges a **₹399 preview fee** before approval ([Huminity](https://www.huminity.in/)). Your free AI preview in 1–2 minutes is a strong hook inside that chat.

**Costs.** Meta does not publish any benchmark for CTWA ads. One roundup found no verified public cost-per-chat figure anywhere ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)). So the best numbers come from Indian agencies' own accounts. GRWW (16 Oct 2026) reports **₹5.43 per chat for a clothing brand**, **₹23.16 for a wedding photographer whose best ad was a Reel**, **₹27–₹65 for wedding studios**, and **₹423 for an interiors firm that got only 2 chats**. It also says Reels "often had the highest CTR", and that price and how carefully people think before buying drive the cost ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)). A Pune practitioner sees **₹20–₹25 per lead** in live campaigns (17 Sep 2026) ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). Vendor planning guides say **₹15–₹60 per chat** ([CodingClave](https://codingclave.com/blog/whatsapp-ads-cost-india-2026)). A figurine at ₹3,599 or more is an emotional but considered gift. So our estimate is **₹20–₹60 per chat** for you, somewhere between the clothing and the wedding-studio numbers. Indian ad space on Meta is cheap: tracked India CPMs average about **US$1.98 (about ₹165) per 1,000 views** ([SuperAds](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india)). Another source says **₹50–₹120** ([OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/meta-ads-benchmarks-india)). Sources disagree, so treat these as rough.

**Use Ads Manager, not the Boost button.** Boosting turns a post into an ad in about 30 seconds. But it gives you less targeting, less reporting and weaker conversion tracking, and it cannot retarget website visitors ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/boost-instagram-posts/)). The Indian CTWA practitioner lists "using the Boost button instead of Ads Manager" as a common mistake ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). Our advice: boost only to push an organic Reel that is already doing well. Run all WhatsApp-lead ads in Ads Manager.

**The setup practitioners use in 2026** is simple. You choose the **Engagement (or Leads) objective** and set the **conversion location to WhatsApp**. You use **one ad set with Advantage+ audience** and leave placements on, so Reels and Stories are included ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). The free WhatsApp Business app, linked to your Facebook Page, "works fine" for this, and most small businesses should start there ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). Common setup failures are a WhatsApp number already linked to another Page, or the Instagram account not connected to the same Page ([Clickatell](https://www.clickatell.com/help-center/whatsapp/marketing-commerce/how-do-you-set-up-your-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)). In one Meta test, ads that let people pick WhatsApp, Instagram DM or Messenger had a **9.5% lower cost per message** than WhatsApp-only ads ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)). But then the chat counts mix three inboxes ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)). Our judgement: start with WhatsApp only, because that is where you take orders, and test the mix later.

**Advantage+ in plain words.** "Advantage+ audience" means you let Meta find buyers instead of picking interests by hand. Use it. "Advantage+ Sales campaigns" (renamed from Advantage+ Shopping around February 2025) are built to optimise for sales ([Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-advantage-plus-shopping-campaigns/)). An Australian personalised-gift shop got **10x ROAS** from one Advantage+ campaign over Easter 2024. But that was one short campaign from an agency blog, for a shop with an online store ([Pup Digital](https://www.pupdigital.com.au/blog/case-study-leveraging-meta-advantage--for-seasonal-success-with-gifts-keepsakes)). Your orders close inside WhatsApp, so Meta cannot see them. Meta only sees your sales if you move to the paid WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) and send each order back through "Conversions API for Business Messaging" ([Meta developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)). Until then, sales-optimised campaigns have no sales data to learn from. That last point is our inference.

**The learning phase decides your minimum budget.** Meta needs about **50 results per ad set in 7 days** to finish learning. A common rule is: daily budget = (cost per result × 50) ÷ 7 ([Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-learning-phase/)). Budget changes above about **20%**, or edits to targeting or the goal, restart learning ([AdMake](https://admakeai.com/blog/facebook-ads-learning-phase-explained)). Here is our arithmetic. If a chat costs ₹60, you need about **₹430 a day** to exit learning when you pay for chats. If one order costs ₹600, you would need about **₹4,300 a day** to learn on orders. That is why you should start by paying for chats.

**Creative that works.** GRWW advises **3 to 6 creatives per ad set**, with at least one vertical video. Show real work in the first seconds, use a specific offer instead of "contact us", and refresh about once a month ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)). IGP's agency ran **reel-only vertical ads** for Raksha Bandhan. It found that longer films cost more per view, so it scaled the short ones ([Social Beat](https://www.socialbeat.in/igp-showcase-page/)). That finding is about views, not sales. One 2026 agency study of DTC ads found that winners showed **a real customer's face in the first second and a price before the call to action** ([Adligator](https://adligator.com/blog/facebook-ads-case-study-breakdown-real-campaigns-2026)). Your making-of and testimonial Reels fit this well. A good order is photo, then preview, then the full-colour printed figurine, then the person's reaction. **Partnership ads** let you run a customer's own tagged post as an ad. Meta's own data reports **19% lower cost per result and 13% higher CTR** than normal ads ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance)). That is a Meta claim, but it costs you little to test with real unboxing videos.

**WhatsApp costs.** The free Business app has no per-message fee. That last point is our understanding, because Meta's price list covers only the paid Platform. On the Platform, Meta has charged per message since **1 July 2025**. A chat that starts from a CTWA ad opens a free window that "may remain open for up to 7 days" ([Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). India marketing messages cost about **₹0.86 each plus 18% GST**. That figure is secondhand, from vendor blogs ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)). From **1 October 2026**, Platform users pay for service replies beyond 1,000 a month per number ([Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)). Stay on the free app until chat volume or the need for sales tracking forces the move.

## Google Search catches ready buyers, but the niche looks small

Google's strength is intent. The person typing "couple figurine from photo" already wants one. A Google and Ipsos study found that **9 in 10 Indian festive shoppers research before buying** ([Zee Biz](https://www.zeebiz.com/technology/news-how-ai-youtube-and-google-search-are-influencing-india-s-festive-purchases-403709)).

**WhatsApp on Google is possible, with limits.** Google's "message assets" add a WhatsApp chat button to Search ads, and work with Search and Performance Max ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14888522?hl=en)). India was one of the first two launch markets, and the feature reached 13 countries on 21 August 2024 ([PPC News Feed](https://ppcnewsfeed.com/ppc-news/2024-08/whatsapp-asset-rolls-out-to-11-more-countries/)). But Google still calls it **beta**. It needs **advertiser verification**, and new advertisers may see "temporarily limited" impressions at first ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14888522?hl=en)). The current help page lists no countries, so India eligibility in 2026 is inferred from the 2024 reports. The main ad headline still sends people to a web page. Google's rules reject pages that are "not crawlable" or do not match the ad ([Google policy help](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9338593?hl=en)). No Google document allows or bans a wa.me link as the main address. So you need a real landing page on yours3dindia.com, with prices, delivery time, reviews and a big WhatsApp button.

**Costs are not known for your keywords.** No public source gives Indian costs or search volumes for "custom 3D figurine", "couple miniature" or "3D printed photo gift". Broad Indian e-commerce estimates are **₹3–₹20 per click** ([Nico Digital, Jun 2026](https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/)) or **₹10–₹40 per click**. One analyst found that three Indian sources give three different answers and advises against averaging them ([Rahul D Sarker](https://rahuldsarker.co/insights/google-ads-cpc-india-by-industry)). The only gift benchmark is American. For "Shopping, Collectibles & Gifts" it shows **CTR 8.28%, US$4.14 per click and a 4.01% conversion rate** (April 2025–March 2026) ([LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/)). Big players such as IGP already advertise on "personalised gifts" in India ([IGP](https://www.igp.com/personalized-gifts)), so broad gift words will likely cost more. Our estimate is ₹15 per click and 3% of page visitors starting a chat (an Indian agency puts a decent page at 2–3% ([Nico Digital](https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/))). That makes **about ₹500 per WhatsApp lead**. That is far above Meta's ₹20–₹60 per chat. But a Google searcher may be a much warmer lead. Only your own numbers will tell. Before spending anything, check real volumes in Google Keyword Planner (free; set location India, currency INR).

**Skip the automated Google campaigns for now.** Google's own product manager called **US$100 a day (about ₹8,300)** the "bare minimum" for a Demand Gen campaign (1 Oct 2026) ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-pm-calls-100-a-day-the-floor-for-a-demand-gen-campaign/)). Google says Performance Max should run **at least 6 weeks** without big changes ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11385582?hl=en)). Smart bidding learns over "typically 1–2 weeks, but sometimes up to 6 weeks" ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724896?hl=en)). Third-party guides say target-CPA bidding needs about **30 conversions a month** ([Nico Digital](https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/)). Shopping ads are a poor fit too. Custom items have no barcode, and Merchant Center checkout needs a cart on your own site ([Merchant Center Help](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13580733)). Shopify merchants report warnings and even a suspension over "customizable goods" ([Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/what-causes-google-merchant-center-to-label-goods-as-customizable/248031)). Those are anecdotes. So run **one tight Search campaign** only. Indian agency guides put a normal small-advertiser start at **₹20,000–₹50,000 a month** ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/google-ads-pricing-india-2026/)).

**Who sees the ads.** Ads go only to logged-in adults (18+) on the Free and Go plans. Paid plans see no ads, and no ads appear next to health, mental health or politics ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-chatgpt-ads-users-india-free-go-plans-chatbot-advertising-free-plans-126082700310_1.html), snippet). India had **more than 100 million weekly ChatGPT users** in February 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-to-start-showing-ads-on-chatgpts-free-and-go-tiers-in-india/)). Nobody knows how many of them shop for figurines.

**How the ads work.** An ad is a short text unit marked "Sponsored", shown below ChatGPT's answer. Indian agency guides describe a 5–6 word headline, one line of text, a logo and a web link ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/chatgpt-ads-guide-india/)); OpenAI's own text did not confirm this. There are no keywords. ChatGPT picks ads from the topic of the conversation, your landing page and "context hints" you write. OpenAI says the hints do not guarantee delivery ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/6-months-into-chatgpt-ads-advertisers-still-dont-know-what-good-looks-like/587583/)). So the format cannot show your best asset, which is the figurine itself on video.

**Early results are weak and hard to trust.** Click rates are low. One estimate is **about 0.9% for chatbot ads against about 6.4% for Google search ads** ([CTI Digital summary](https://www.ctidigital.com/insights/chatgpt-ads-campaign-insights/)). Reported US costs run **US$3–$22 per click** ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/6-months-into-chatgpt-ads-advertisers-still-dont-know-what-good-looks-like/587583/)). Hostinger found that "more specific use cases performed better, while broader messaging consistently struggled" ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/6-months-into-chatgpt-ads-advertisers-still-dont-know-what-good-looks-like/587583/)). One advertiser's dashboard showed **120 clicks, but their analytics showed only 8 visits**. We saw this Reddit thread only through a third-party summary ([Tru Commerce](https://trucommerce.ai/insights/chatgpt-ads-reddit-advertiser-reports)). The only Indian figure comes from one agency account over a few days: **CPM ₹581, ₹13.67 per click, CTR 4.25%**. The author says it is "not an industry benchmark" ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/chatgpt-ads-guide-india/)). At that price, ₹725 buys about 50 clicks a day (our arithmetic). Many of the positive reports come from firms that sell ChatGPT-ad services.

**ChatGPT shopping is separate, and you cannot buy it.** Product results in ChatGPT are organic. Instant Checkout started with US merchants and was scaled back around March 2026, according to vendor blogs ([stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/openai-scales-back-instant-checkout)). We found nothing confirming that Indian sellers can join. The free way to show up is a crawlable website with clear product pages, prices and reviews.

**Verdict:** ChatGPT ads are open to you, and nothing suggests figurines are a banned category. OpenAI says early ads focus on "household and consumer goods" ([adtechradar](https://adtechradar.com/2026/03/22/openai-chatgpt-ad-policies/)). But today the product means text-only ads, a required website, weak tracking and no Indian proof. Wait. When your landing page exists and Meta is making money, a capped **two-week test of about ₹10,000–₹15,000** is reasonable. Write context hints around specific gift moments, such as "wedding anniversary gift from photo India".

## Festivals, junk chats and account bans are where money leaks

**Spend before the festival, not on it.** IGP's founder says **Raksha Bandhan, Valentine's Day, Diwali and Mother's Day "drive large spikes"** (26 Aug 2026) ([Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/interview/retail-people/eretail/igp-how-indias-gifting-economy-moving-beyond-occasions)). Meta's 2026 festive playbook stretches the season from pre-Diwali through the **November–February wedding season**, Christmas, year-end and Republic Day sales ([CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/metas-new-festive-playbook-helps-brands-turn-diwali-momentum-into-a-five-month-growth-opportunity/)). This is good news for you. Weddings and couple gifts are your core. A figurine also takes making time plus up to 7 days of delivery. So ads for each festival should start **2–4 weeks early**, and the ads and chat greeting should show a clear last order date. That timing is our inference, not a sourced rule. Prices likely rise in festive weeks as big brands bid, but we found no Indian source that measures this. Last-minute buyers who want same-day delivery are a poor fit for you.

**Cheap chats are not orders.** GRWW warns that many people only ask the price, and that Ads Manager counts chats, not bookings ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)). Practitioners list these mistakes: a generic "Hi" pre-filled message that hides which ad brought the chat; launching before the greeting and quick replies are ready; editing the ad set daily; nobody watching WhatsApp during ad hours; and mixing ad leads with personal chats on one number ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). In one Indian case, adding qualification questions raised qualified leads from **22% to 37%** of chats ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)). Your free preview is also a filter. Someone who sends a photo is a real lead.

**Protect the ad account.** Meta may move Indian accounts to prepaid payment because of RBI rules, which can pause campaigns for a while ([Velocity](https://blog.velocity.in/facebook-ads-blocked-it-could-be-because-of-the-rbis-new-guidelines/)). Failed payments, a card name that does not match, and frequent card changes are reported triggers for disabled accounts ([Pixelscan](https://pixelscan.net/blog/how-to-recover-your-disabled-instagram-ad-account/)). Disabled accounts are a serious problem in India. Agency Blink Digital sued Meta in the Bombay High Court over four disabled accounts, with a reported ₹1,000 crore claim ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=573487)). Use one card in the owner's name, add a second admin, and avoid big budget jumps on a new account.

**Know your break-even before you start.** Indian agencies use **break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ margin** ([Madsun Media](https://www.madsunmedia.com/blog/meta-roas-benchmarks-india-fashion-d2c)). Digital ads in India carry **18% GST** ([Taxgarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-advertising-services-google-meta-ads-rcm-india-2026)); ask your CA whether you can claim it back. Here is our own arithmetic. It **assumes 45% of the price goes on making, packing, shipping and fees**. Replace that with your real number.

| Product | Price | Money left before ads (est.) | Break-even cost per order (est.) | Break-even if GST is a real cost (est.) | Safe target cost per order (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Single | ₹3,599 | ₹1,980 | ₹1,980 | ₹1,680 | ₹850–₹1,300 |
| Couple 13 cm | ₹5,999 | ₹3,300 | ₹3,300 | ₹2,800 | ₹1,400–₹2,200 |
| Couple 18 cm | ₹7,999 | ₹4,400 | ₹4,400 | ₹3,730 | ₹1,900–₹2,900 |

The "safe target" is half to two-thirds of break-even. That is a rule of thumb, not a sourced benchmark. Indian agencies say cash-on-delivery orders are refused **20–35%** of the time, against under 8% for prepaid ([6S Marketers](https://6smarketers.com/d2c-skincare-meta-ads-case-study/)). A refused custom figurine is nearly a total loss, so keep orders prepaid or take an advance.

## A ₹1,500-a-day starting plan

This plan is our synthesis of the sources above. The budget sizes are judgement, not data. If money is tight, run only the Meta part at about ₹700 a day. That is still above the ₹300–₹500 a day the Indian practitioner suggests ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)).

**Week 0: get ready before spending.** Link the WhatsApp Business number to your Facebook Page, and link Instagram to the same Page. Write an instant greeting that shows "Singles from ₹3,599", plus quick replies for the price list, sizes and the delivery promise. Make a different pre-filled message for each ad, for example "Hi, I want a free preview. Ad: COUPLE-REEL-1. Occasion: ___". Start a simple sheet with these columns: date, ad tag, photo sent (yes/no), preview sent, order, amount. On yours3dindia.com, build one fast mobile page with before-and-after photos, prices, 7-day delivery, reviews and a large WhatsApp button. Install the Google tag on it. Complete Google advertiser verification. Check Keyword Planner volumes for your main phrases.

| Platform | Daily budget | Share | What to run |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta, main ad set | ₹700 | 47% | Engagement objective, WhatsApp as conversion location, Advantage+ audience, all India, automatic placements; 4–5 vertical Reels: 2 making-of, 2 testimonial or unboxing, 1 couple price ad |
| Meta, test ad set | ₹300 | 20% | Partnership ads from customers' tagged posts, or a boost of your best organic Reel |
| Google Search | ₹500 | 33% | One campaign; exact and phrase match on themes like "custom figurine from photo", "3D miniature couple", "personalised couple figurine"; WhatsApp message asset and call asset; Search Partners and Display turned off; India; manual bids capped at ₹20–₹30 for 2–3 weeks |
| ChatGPT ads | ₹0 | 0% | Not yet; a ₹10,000–₹15,000 test later, once the landing page works |
| **Total** | **₹1,500** | | About ₹45,000 a month, about ₹53,100 with 18% GST |

The Google settings come from the research notes' starter setup. It adds negative keywords such as free, DIY, "how to make", STL, printer, course, Amazon and Flipkart. Switch Google to "Maximise conversions" on the WhatsApp click only after 2–3 weeks. Move to target CPA only after about 30 conversions a month ([Nico Digital](https://www.nicodigital.com/performance-marketing/google-ads-cost-in-india-cpc-benchmarks/)). On niche words, Google may not spend the full ₹500. That is fine. Do not widen to broad match just to spend the money.

**Days 1–14: hands off.** Do not edit the ad sets. Practitioners say to wait about a week before judging, and to run for two weeks ([Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)). Our estimate: at ₹20–₹60 per chat, the Meta main ad set should bring roughly 160–490 chats in 14 days. Reply fast, during ad hours too. On Google, check the search terms twice a week and add negatives.

**Week 3 onward: move money toward orders.** Pause Meta ads that cost more than twice the median cost per chat. Raise budgets in steps of **20% or less** to avoid restarting learning ([AdMake](https://admakeai.com/blog/facebook-ads-learning-phase-explained)). Move money between Meta and Google based on **cost per order**. Cost per chat is not enough. Refresh creatives about once a month ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)). Before Valentine's Day and each festival, have new occasion Reels ready 2–3 weeks early. When volume grows, move to the WhatsApp Cloud API through a provider and send orders back to Meta. Only then try Meta's purchase-optimised campaigns.

**Metrics to track every week.**

| Metric | How to get it | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per chat | Ads Manager (Meta); conversions on the WhatsApp click (Google) | Planning range ₹20–₹60 on Meta (estimate) |
| Photo rate: chats that send a photo | Your sheet | The real lead count; no benchmark exists |
| Cost per photo lead | Spend ÷ photo leads | Compare Meta with Google on this, not on chats |
| Order rate: orders ÷ chats | Your sheet, by ad tag | Unknown; the notes used 5% and 20–30% only as guesses |
| Cost per order | Spend (plus GST) ÷ orders | Keep it under the safe target in the table above |
| Average order value | Your sheet | Couples raise what you can afford to pay per order |
| Return on ad spend | Order revenue ÷ spend | Must beat about 1.8x (about 2.1x with GST) at a 45% cost (estimate) |
| Reply time | WhatsApp | Slow replies waste ad money ([GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)) |

## What the research could not find

These gaps are real, and you should know them before you spend. **Reddit could not be opened** by the research tools, so no Reddit seller threads were read. The one Reddit-based point above came through a third-party summary. **No source gives a cost per order for any figurine, miniature or caricature seller**, in India or abroad. **No Indian gifting brand publishes its ROAS or cost per order.** No source gives Indian search volumes or click costs for figurine keywords, and Google Trends could not be pulled. No India figure exists for how much ad prices rise in festive weeks. No source gives a chat-to-order rate for WhatsApp gift leads. OpenAI's own pages were blocked (HTTP 403), so the ChatGPT India facts come from TechCrunch, Business Standard snippets and agency blogs. We could not confirm whether ChatGPT ads accept a WhatsApp link, or whether Indian sellers can join ChatGPT shopping. Meta's official learning-phase page and India rate card were not read directly, so those figures are secondhand. We did not check any competitor's ads in the Meta Ad Library. A free manual search there (country India; "miniature", "3D figurine", "caricature") would show what rivals run today.

## Conclusion

The question is less "which platform is best" and more "which platform can see your sale". Today none of them can, because the sale happens inside WhatsApp. That is why your own sheet, with an ad tag on every chat, matters more than any dashboard. It is also why the free WhatsApp preview is worth more than any targeting setting. The preview turns a cheap Meta chat into a real lead, and it is the first proof of quality for a buyer who has never met you. Meta leads because a Reel can show the transformation, from photo to figurine to a happy face, and a text ad on Google or ChatGPT cannot.

Two things would change this advice. A working landing page on yours3dindia.com would make Google, and later ChatGPT, fair to judge. Moving to the WhatsApp Cloud API and sending orders back to Meta would let Meta learn from buyers instead of chatters. Do the first now and the second when chats grow. Revisit ChatGPT ads after the wedding season, when Indian advertisers will have had about six months of data and OpenAI may have published its India rules.
