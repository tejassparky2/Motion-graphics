# What to record for weeks 1-2 (2-15 Nov 2026)

Put everything in one Google Drive folder ("Anyone with the link"), with one subfolder per reel (A, B, C...).
Send original files from the phone or PC, not WhatsApp copies.

## How to record (all clips)
- Phone **vertical**, on a stand or tripod. 4K 30 fps (or 1080p 60 fps). Clean the lens.
- Soft daylight from a window, or one soft light. Plain, tidy background.
- **iPhone:** turn off HDR video (Settings → Camera → Record Video → HDR Video: off).
- Each clip 5-20 seconds. Start recording 2 seconds before the action and stop 2 seconds after.
- Screen recordings: hide notifications, and make sure no customer names or phone numbers are visible.
- No music, text or filters. I add all of that.

## Week 1 (please send by Thu 30 Oct)

### A. Photo → 3D figurine in 60 s (full shot list: `reel07/shot_list.md`)
Film one real figurine from start to finish (with the customer's OK, or a family member's).
1. The original phone photo (the file itself).
2. Screen recording: ChatGPT. Upload the photo, type the prompt, the design appears.
3. Screen recording: Tripo3D. Upload the design, the model builds, the 3D model spins.
4. Screen recording: Bambu Studio. Painting the colours, one full unedited run (also used for reel E).
5. Screenshot: the sliced model with print time and filament.
6. Printer: 3 clips of 10-15 s (first layers, middle, finished). A timelapse is even better.
7. Your hands removing supports and sanding: 20-30 s.
8. **The hook shot:** the finished figurine held next to the phone showing the original photo, with a slow turn, 10 s.

### B. Bambu Lab A1 vs Snapmaker U1
Same model, same size, same filament brand on both printers.
1. Screenshots from both slicers: print time, filament used, waste (flush/prime tower) and number of colour changes.
2. Both printers running side by side: one wide shot, 10 s.
3. A1 close-up: one colour change, with the cut and the purge ("poop") coming out, 10-15 s.
4. U1 close-up: one tool swap, 10 s.
5. After printing: each printer's waste on a kitchen scale, with the grams clearly visible. Film each separately.
6. Both finished figurines side by side, plus close-ups of face and colour edges, 10 s each, in the same light.
7. **Your verdict** in 1-2 sentences (text message is fine).
