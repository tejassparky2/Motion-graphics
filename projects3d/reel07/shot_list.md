# Reel 07 shot list: "How to make a photo into a 3D figurine"

The first learning reel (week 1, posts Mon 2 Nov, 7–9 PM IST). Script: `plan.md`. Film **one new figurine** from start to finish. The
person in the photo (or the parent, for a child) must agree to the photo and figurine being shown. Keep customer names
and phone numbers off every screen.

**To post on Monday, send everything by Saturday 1 Nov night.** If it comes later, reel 06 (ready) goes out on Monday
and this one moves to Wednesday.

## Setup for camera shots
- Phone **vertical**, on a stand or tripod, 4K 30 fps. Clean the lens.
- **iPhone:** HDR video off (Settings → Camera → Record Video → HDR Video).
- Soft window light, plain tidy background, the same spot for every figurine shot.
- Keep the figurine in the **middle of the frame**, with empty space above and below (the text goes there).
- Start recording 2 s before the action and stop 2 s after. No music, text or filters.

## Setup for screen recordings
- **PC:** Windows Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS; Mac Cmd + Shift + 5. Browser zoom 125% so the text is readable on a phone.
- **Phone:** Control Centre → Screen Recording, with Do Not Disturb on.
- Hide the chat history sidebar, your account name or email, other tabs and notifications.
- Move the mouse slowly. Record the **full run unedited**, even if it takes minutes; I speed it up.
