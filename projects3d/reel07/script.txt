Step two. We give that design to Tripo 3D, and it builds a full 3D model.

The colours aren't perfect yet. So, step three. In the slicer, we paint the parts it missed, like the eyes.

Step four. We print it on the Snapmaker U one. Eleven hours. Nine hundred and fifty colour changes.
