# 3D explainer shorts

Vertical (1080×1920) 3D explainer videos in the style of popular "what happens when…" shorts:
a character, an x-ray view, a medical cutaway, bold captions, narration and sound design.
All characters, models, animation, sound and captions are generated by code in Blender 4.5.

## Test short: "What happens when you crack your knuckles?"

| shot | time | what happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1. Crack | 0–3.5 s | A guy presses his fist into his palm and his knuckles crack. |
| 2. X-ray | 3.5–5.8 s | Close-up on his open hand; the skin fades to x-ray, the joints glow on "fluid". |
| 3. Cutaway | 5.8–10.6 s | Half-section of a knuckle: bone, cartilage, capsule and synovial fluid. The bones pull apart, dissolved gas gathers and a bubble pops into being. |
| 4. Smile | 10.6–12.1 s | "That's the pop!" A satisfied grin. |
| 5. Again | 12.1–16 s | He tries again and nothing happens; a "20 MIN" stopwatch appears. |

## Where everything comes from

- **Character:** [MPFB2](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/) (MakeHuman for Blender, GPL add-on).
  Characters it generates and the MakeHuman system assets, skins and ARKit face units (`faceunits01`) are **CC0**.
  `xb/character.py` builds the character; `xb/posing.py` adds arm IK, hand shapes and eye tracking;
  `xb/anim.py` keys the performance (hands, head, gaze, 52 face units, blinks).
- **Room, x-ray and cutaway:** procedural (`xb/room.py`, `xb/xray.py`, `knuckles/joint.py`).
- **Voice:** [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) text-to-speech (Apache-2.0), with word timings for captions.
- **Captions:** Montserrat Black (SIL Open Font License), rendered with Pillow (`xb/captions.py`).
- **Sound and music:** synthesised with numpy/scipy (`knuckles/sound.py`).

## Reproduce

```bash
# 1. Blender 4.5 LTS + MPFB2 + CC0 asset packs
blender -b --command extension install-file -r user_default -e add-on-mpfb-v2.0.18.zip
# unzip makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip, faceunits01.zip and skins01_cc0.zip into
# ~/.config/blender/4.5/extensions/.user/user_default/mpfb/data/

# 2. Voice (Python 3.12 venv with kokoro + soundfile + en_core_web_sm)
python explainer/knuckles/voice.py out/audio

# 3. Scene, frames, captions, sound, video
blender -b -P explainer/knuckles/build.py -- --out out/knuckles.blend
blender -b out/knuckles.blend -P pelican/tools/render_frames.py -- out/frames --range 1-384 --pct 100
python3 explainer/xb/captions.py out/audio/words.json out/captions --frames 384 --timer 13.95
python3 explainer/xb/post.py out/frames out/captions out/final --post out/knuckles_post.json --zoom 5.42 5.75
$BLENDER_PY explainer/knuckles/sound.py out/audio/voice.wav out/audio/mix.wav
ffmpeg -framerate 24 -i out/final/f_%04d.png -i out/audio/mix.wav -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
       -c:a aac -b:a 192k -shortest knuckles.mp4
```
