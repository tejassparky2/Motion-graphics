# News & Tech channel: format study and first five ideas (researched 4 Oct 2026)

## Format notes (inspiration only, nothing copied)
Omar Agamy (Egyptian-Canadian, started on TikTok in early 2022, YouTube since April 2022) makes short vertical videos
about countries of the world: fun facts and recent developments, one idea per video.
Sources: https://youtube.fandom.com/wiki/Omar_Agamy , https://www.youtube.com/@omaragamyy
What we take from the *format*: the surprising claim in the first second, a map or flag on screen straight away, one
fact or development per video, plain words, about 40-60 s, end on a question. Our look, voice, characters and wording
stay our own.

## Five candidate videos (two or more sources each; dates checked)

### 1. Google put AI chips in orbit (tech)
- 1 Oct 2026: a prototype satellite built with Planet, carrying four Google TPUs (AI chips), launched on SpaceX
  Transporter-18 from Vandenberg. First step of "Project Suncatcher": could AI data centres run on solar power in space?
- Fridge-sized, about 1 kW of solar power. Tests radiation, heat and launch stress. Two more satellites planned next year
  to test laser links. Sceptics: cooling (chips run about 15 min before cooling down), cost, no repairs in orbit.
- Sources: Google blog https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-prototype/ ;
  NPR https://kvcr.org/news/npr-top-stories/2026-10-01/project-suncatcher-google-ai-data-center-space ;
  Google on X https://x.com/Google/status/2105803583648100611

### 2. A king goes home after 1,000 years (world/history)
- 3 Oct 2026: Greece handed Bulgaria the remains attributed to Tsar Samuel (ruled 997-1014), found in 1965 on the island of
  Agios Achilleios, Little Prespa Lake. In return Bulgaria gave back 48 church objects taken from northern Greece in WWI.
  Talks began in 2007. Ceremony in Thessaloniki with both prime ministers, then a state ceremony in Sofia.
- Story hook: chroniclers say Samuel died of shock after Basil II sent his captured soldiers back blinded (1014).
  Say "chroniclers say"; the number of soldiers is disputed, so don't state one.
- Sources: AFP via France 24 https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261003-bulgaria-s-tsar-samuel-returns-home-after-1-000-years ;
  AP via Courthouse News https://www.courthousenews.com/remains-of-medieval-king-czar-samuel-arrive-in-bulgaria-after-decades-of-talks-with-greece/ ;
  BTA https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/1213569-greece-to-return-remains-of-tsar-samuel-to-bulgaria

### 3. Argentina will sell citizenship (world/money)
- 2 Oct 2026: Economy Minister Luis Caputo announced South America's first citizenship-by-investment programme:
  US$350,000 non-refundable contribution or US$800,000 in a public bond; spouse / adult children $100,000, under-18s
  $25,000; no residence requirement; security checks; applications open Q4 2026.
- Fairness: give the government's reason (attract investment) and the usual criticism of "golden passports"
  (e.g. EU concerns about security and money laundering) with a source for that before scripting.
- Sources: Reuters via CP24 https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/10/02/argentina-announces-citizenship-by-investment-program/ ;
  Bloomberg https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/argentina-offers-golden-passports-to-foreigners-for-350-000

### 4. Japan's permanent residency fee went up 20 times (world/countries)
- From 1 Oct 2026 the permanent-residency fee rose from ¥10,000 to ¥200,000, with a higher income bar; wider rules
  (pension record, Japanese-language and society knowledge) are phased in, the broader framework from 1 Apr 2027.
- Sources disagree on some details: confirm each number with the Immigration Services Agency (moj.go.jp/isa) before scripting.
- Sources: Japan Times https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/10/01/japan/permanent-residency-guidelines-revision/ ;
  Nikkei Asia https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/japan-immigration/japan-plans-to-tighten-permanent-residency-requirements-for-foreigners ;
  Tokyo Weekender https://www.tokyoweekender.com/japan-life/news-and-opinion/permanent-residency-rules-explained-2026/

### 5. Hawaii's 90-foot sea arch is gone (world/nature)
- Hōlei Sea Arch, Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, end of Chain of Craters Road: standing on 23 Sep 2026, gone by the night
  of 27 Sep; exact time unknown, possibly during the park's closure for storm Nolo. Weakened by the 2018 Kīlauea earthquakes,
  new cracks in 2020, and surf damage. Cut from an old lava flow (NPS older page says about 550 years; confirm).
- Outlets differ on "Hurricane" vs "Tropical Storm" Nolo: use NPS wording.
- Sources: NPS https://www.nps.gov/havo/learn/news/20261001-nr-holei-sea-arch_collapses.htm ;
  Hawaii News Now https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/10/01/end-road-famed-90-foot-holei-sea-arch-collapses-hawaii-island/ ;
  NBC News https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/hawaiis-picturesque-holei-sea-arch-collapses-ocean-rcna601241
