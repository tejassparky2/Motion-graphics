## Q3. Musical, vocal and lyrical characteristics

### Takeaway
Very little technical data (tempo, key) was found. Sources describe (A) as theatrical, story-led pop by a singer whose newer work adds acoustic guitar and folk. (B) has a confessional, letter-to-God structure about grief.

### Cited Findings
- (A) is called "theatrical", from an artist who started in melancholy R&B and hip-hop and moved toward acoustic and folk elements. — [iHeart](https://www.iheart.com/artist/marino-50436921/)
- (A) lyrical device: a narrative parable with a reversal of who the threat is; the devil is a charming stranger, not a monster. Songfacts places it beside other "devil in disguise" songs (Elvis, Carrie Underwood's "Cowboy Casanova", Kanye West's "Devil In A New Dress"). — [Songfacts](https://dev-www.songfacts.com/facts/marino/devil-in-disguise)
- (B) lyrical device: an imagined letter or prayer to God, with denial lines ("not angry, just tired"; not asking for money) that build toward the real wish, to see or say goodbye to a lost person. — [blinkboxmusic](https://blinkboxmusic.com/2026/10/makna-lagu-if-i-could-write-to-god-vex-woven-lirik-terjemahan/)
- (B) mood conflict: one repost blog calls it upbeat with a "vibrant sound", which clashes with every other description. It reads as generic filler. — [faveloaded (search result, not opened)](https://www.faveloaded.com/vex-woven-if-i-could-write-to-god/)

### Inferences
- The shared device is **negation then reveal**: "you think X, but it's Y" in (A), and "I'm not X, I'm Y" or "I wouldn't ask for X" in (B). That is the same pattern as this channel's twist hooks, and each half fits one caption card.

### Gaps
- No BPM, key or production credits beyond "self-produced" for (A). No vocal or production information for (B).

## Q4. 2024-2026 patterns: sad/dark lyric edits, anime edits, AI songs

### Takeaway
Anime edit trends attach to one syncable lyric moment and spread as the same sound is reused on new characters. In 2025-26, masked or anonymous artists, some using Suno on their vocals, have been going viral on TikTok, and listeners often don't notice or don't care.

### Cited Findings
- Dominic Fike "Mama's Boy" edit trend: began with a 17 Jul 2023 TikTok using "stylized subtitles". Edits sync to the spelled-out ending and swap in characters (Miles Morales, Eren Yeager, Tomura Shigaraki). One edit reached about 1.6M plays in 18 days. The main sound had over 30,900 posts by 11 Sep 2023. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/dominic-fikes-mamas-boy-edit-trend)
- Dazed (8 Oct 2026), "Suno is the new auto-tune":
  - Masked artists (Kilo Kent, "40's"; Zemi) use Suno to change or mask their own sung vocals while keeping their own beats and toplines.
  - Both are signed to Isekai Records, a "next-gen label" that turns social hype into streams.
  - TikTok commenters suspect AI, but many fans don't care.
  - Haven's "I Run" was pulled by Spotify in Nov 2025 over alleged AI impersonation of Jorja Smith, re-released with a human vocalist, and now has over 250M streams.
  - Zemi admits fully generated songs were noticeable to listeners.
  - [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/71134/1/suno-is-the-new-auto-tune-meet-the-artists-using-ai-to-go-viral)
- Anonymity and masks are a recurring part of the persona, which makes it hard to tell human from AI music. — [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/71134/1/suno-is-the-new-auto-tune-meet-the-artists-using-ai-to-go-viral)

### Inferences
- Edit-friendly songs share four traits: a single line that lands a reveal, clean lines that work as captions, an emotional or dark tone that fits a character arc, and a masked or faceless artist, so the edit's character becomes the "face".
- For this channel: using these songs in posted videos would raise copyright and Content ID questions (Marino's song is a commercial release). A Suno-generated track carries licensing terms that depend on the plan. Original music modelled on the devices above is safer. Not researched here.

### Gaps
- No Know Your Meme entry or article found specifically on "sad lyric edit" songs or glowing word-by-word lyric styles. No TikTok Creative Center data was retrieved.
