# Four scripts for approval (researched 5 Oct 2026, news from 1–5 Oct 2026)

Every fact below has at least two sources (listed per script). Dates were checked: search engines returned several
2025 stories dated as 2026 (e.g. the "100% pharma tariff from Oct 1" is from 2025), so those were dropped.

## Retention rules used in every script
- Line 1 (first ~3 s) is the hook: a "you" angle or a surprising number, with a strong visual. Most viewers decide
  in the first seconds whether to swipe (creator analyses: joinbrands.com, virvid.ai, aibrify.com, 2026).
- An open question early ("Will I actually pay less?") that is only answered near the end, so viewers stay.
- One fact per line, short sentences, a new picture on screen about every 2 seconds.
- A payoff or "catch" at the end, then a comment question (the last screen loops back to the first idea).
- 40–50 s. US angle first: prices, jobs, safety, "what it means for you" (Pew 2026: gas prices are Americans' top
  worry about the Iran war).
