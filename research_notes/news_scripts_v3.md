# News scripts v3: hooks, value all the way through, a comment question at the end (draft for the owner's OK)

Rules and research: `research_notes/news_engagement.md`.
- Each line was voiced with the channel voice and checked with Whisper medium. Every line was heard correctly; the
  only differences were numbers written as digits.
- Lengths below include the new pacing.
- "Tell me in the comments" moves to the screen, next to the YES/NO buttons and in the pinned comment.
- New facts are checked:
  - CDC: plague spreads mostly by flea bites, and fleas come from wild rodents such as squirrels and prairie dogs.
    If you have symptoms, seek medical attention right away.
  - BLS: the next jobs report is on 6 Nov 2026.
  - macOS: Full Disk Access is in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

## Plague (WORLD): about 59 s
1. *Hook:* Doctors suspect the plague killed a lab worker in Siberia.
2. *Explains a term or number:* The plague is the same disease that caused the Black Death in the fourteenth century.
3. *Why it matters to you:* And it matters to Americans too.
4. *Story step:* The worker was a twenty-eight-year-old technician at a lab in Irkutsk, Russia, that studies the plague.
5. *Story step:* She went to hospital on September twenty-ninth and died a few days later.
6. *Story step:* Officials isolated one hundred ninety-seven people who may have had contact with her.
7. *Story step:* But the plague has not been confirmed.
8. *Story step:* Russia's health agency says she died of pneumonia with an unknown cause.
9. *Signpost:* Now, here is why it matters to Americans.
10. *Story step:* The plague still infects about seven people in the United States every year, mostly in the West.
11. *Story step:* People usually catch it from the bite of a flea that lived on a wild animal, like a squirrel or a prairie dog.
12. *Story step:* The good news is that antibiotics can treat it when it is caught early.
13. *What happens next / what you can do:* So if you fall sick after contact with wild animals in the West, see a doctor quickly.
14. *Comment question:* Did you know the plague still exists in America?

## Jobs (US NEWS): about 53 s
1. *Hook:* America added only twenty-nine thousand jobs in September.
2. *Story step:* Economists expected about ninety thousand, so the official number was less than a third of that.
3. *Why it matters to you:* If you are looking for work, this matters to you.
4. *Story step:* The unemployment rate also rose, from four point one to four point two percent.
5. *Explains a term or number:* That means about four in every hundred people in the workforce are out of work and looking for a job.
6. *Signpost:* Pay is growing slowly too.
7. *Story step:* Average hourly pay rose three percent over the past year, the slowest rise since twenty twenty-one.
8. *Signpost:* So where are the new jobs?
9. *Story step:* Most of them were in health care, with a few more in construction and in factories.
10. *Story step:* Economists call this a low hire, low fire job market.
11. *Story step:* Companies are not laying many people off, but they are not hiring many people either.
12. *What happens next / what you can do:* The next jobs report comes out on November sixth, and we will tell you what it shows.
13. *Comment question:* Is it harder to find a job where you live?

## Japan (WORLD): about 55 s
1. *Hook:* Japan just made staying in the country for good twenty times more expensive.
2. *Story step:* On October first, the fee for permanent residency jumped from ten thousand yen to two hundred thousand yen.
3. *Explains a term or number:* That is a jump from about sixty-three dollars to about twelve hundred and seventy dollars.
4. *Why it matters to you:* So if you ever dreamed of moving to Japan, here is what you need to know.
5. *Explains a term or number:* Permanent residency lets a foreigner stay in Japan with no time limit.
6. *Story step:* But it does not make them a citizen, and it does not come with a Japanese passport.
7. *Story step:* Japan now has more than four million foreign residents, which is a record.
8. *Signpost:* So why did Japan raise the fee so much?
9. *Story step:* The government says the new fee covers the real cost of handling applications.
10. *Story step:* It also says the fee is now closer to what other countries charge.
11. *Signpost:* And there is one more change.
12. *Story step:* Applicants now also need a higher income to qualify.
13. *Comment question:* Would you pay twelve hundred dollars to live in Japan for good?

## Diesel (US NEWS): about 68 s
1. *Hook:* Diesel just hit its highest price ever in the United States.
2. *Story step:* On September twenty-second, the average gallon cost six dollars and fifty-two cents.
3. *Why it matters to you:* Even if you never buy diesel, you are probably paying for it.
4. *Story step:* The trucks that bring food and goods to your stores run on diesel.
5. *Story step:* So when diesel costs more, almost everything in the store can cost more too.
6. *Signpost:* Why did the price jump so much?
7. *Story step:* The war with Iran has stopped most oil tankers from passing through the Strait of Hormuz.
8. *Explains a term or number:* That narrow sea route normally carries about one in every five barrels of oil the world uses.
9. *Story step:* With less oil getting through, diesel became scarce all around the world.
10. *Story step:* On October second, the G7, a group of seven of the world's biggest economies, agreed on a plan.
11. *Story step:* Together, they will release up to one hundred million barrels of oil and diesel from their emergency reserves.
12. *Story step:* Energy experts say diesel could fall by about twenty-five cents a gallon within a few weeks.
13. *Signpost:* But there is a catch.
14. *Story step:* Emergency reserves are a short-term fix, and they will have to be refilled later.
15. *What happens next / what you can do:* So prices may drop soon, but they may not stay low for long.
16. *Comment question:* Have you noticed higher prices at the store?

## Apple (TECH): about 50 s
1. *Hook:* If you use a Mac, Apple is changing a setting that lets apps see your private files.
2. *Explains a term or number:* The setting is called Full Disk Access.
3. *Story step:* When you turn it on for an app, that app can see almost everything on your Mac.
4. *Story step:* That includes your files, your emails, your messages and even your browsing history.
5. *Story step:* Many new AI helpers ask for this access, so they can work for you in the background.
6. *Story step:* On October second, Apple warned app makers that this could expose everything on your computer without your full knowledge and understanding.
7. *Story step:* So Apple plans to make this setting harder to turn on.
8. *Story step:* You will need to take a very clear and deliberate step before an app gets that access.
9. *Story step:* Apple has not said when this change will arrive.
10. *What happens next / what you can do:* Until then, you can see which apps already have it in System Settings, under Privacy and Security.
11. *Comment question:* Would you let an AI read everything on your computer?

## Tesla (TECH): about 54 s
1. *Hook:* Tesla's taxi with no steering wheel just finished its first paid month.
2. *Explains a term or number:* It is called the Cybercab, and it carries passengers around Austin, Texas, with no driver.
3. *Explains a term or number:* It has no pedals either, so nobody inside can take control.
4. *Signpost:* It was a bumpy start.
5. *Story step:* Passengers complained about long waits and about cars picking them up in the wrong spots.
6. *Story step:* Some also had problems with the doors and the trunk.
7. *Story step:* So far, no major crashes have been reported.
8. *Story step:* But federal safety regulators have opened a review of the car.
9. *Story step:* Austin firefighters have also asked for clear rules on how to handle a car with no driver controls.
10. *Signpost:* Even so, Tesla is not slowing down.
11. *Story step:* The number of Cybercabs registered in Texas has grown from forty-five at launch to one hundred sixty-nine.
12. *Explains a term or number:* That is almost four times as many.
13. *Story step:* Elon Musk says the company is being extremely careful with safety.
14. *Comment question:* Would you ride in a car with no steering wheel?
