# Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads and Click-to-WhatsApp for Yours3D India (custom 3D-printed figurines, ₹3,599 to ₹7,999)

Research date: 21 Oct 2026. Most numeric benchmarks are from Indian agencies, vendor blogs or tracking tools, not from Meta. Meta does not publish India or industry benchmarks for click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads (see Q1). No gifting or figurine-specific CTWA benchmark was found. Every figure below carries its source and date. Lines marked "inference" or "estimate" are my arithmetic or judgement, not sourced facts.

## Q1. Typical India CPM, CPC, CTR and cost per messaging conversation for CTWA (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
No authoritative India benchmark exists, and Meta publishes none. Indian agency data from 2026 puts CTWA cost per conversation started anywhere from under ₹3 to about ₹65, and one interiors account hit ₹423. The planning range most sources agree on is roughly ₹15 to ₹60 per conversation. India Meta CPMs are among the cheapest in the world (about ₹50 to ₹250 depending on the source), and CPCs run about ₹8 to ₹34. What matters is cost per qualified chat and cost per order, not cost per chat.

### Cited Findings
- Meta publishes no industry benchmarks for CTWA ads. The only verified figures are Meta's A/B tests. One roundup found no verified public CTR, CPM or cost-per-conversation benchmark for CTWA in India or anywhere else (retrieved Oct 2026). — [Spur, CTWA benchmarks](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- Indian agency GRWW (16 Oct 2026) reports actual cost per conversation from its own accounts:
  - home-improvement: ₹2.72 (153 chats)
  - clothing brand: ₹5.43 (2,564 enquiries; best ad ₹3.35)
  - wedding photographer, Patna, best ad a Reel: ₹23.16
  - wedding studios: ₹27 to ₹65 (best ads ₹43)
  - wedding film studio: ₹42.37
  - interiors: ₹423 (only 2 chats)

  CTRs were 3.50% for a Reel and 3.63% for another messaging ad. It says Reels "often had the highest CTR", and that product price and how considered the purchase is drive the cost. — [GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)
- A Pune practitioner (17 Sep 2026) sees about ₹20 to ₹25 per lead from CTWA in live campaigns and calls it a working range, not a promise. The same source puts Instant Form leads at ₹20 to ₹250 depending on category. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)
- Vendor and agency planning ranges (2026, not sourced to datasets):
  - ₹5 to ₹25 per conversation for e-commerce
  - ₹15 to ₹80 for real estate and education
  - ₹40 to ₹150 for B2B
  - ₹15 to ₹60 per chat opener, said to be often 30 to 40% cheaper per lead than website forms

  — [Xtrodinry](https://www.xtrodinry.com/blog/whatsapp-ads-cost-india), [Adzbe](https://www.adzbe.com/blog/whatsapp-click-to-message-ads-cost-setup-2026), [CodingClave](https://codingclave.com/blog/whatsapp-ads-cost-india-2026)
- India case on Instagram DM ads, not WhatsApp: House Hunt spent ₹1,26,000 for 3,800 message starts (about ₹33 per chat) and 840 qualified leads (₹150 per qualified lead, 22% qualified). After adding qualification it got about ₹36 per chat and ₹98 per qualified lead (37% qualified) over 14 days. — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- India Meta CPM and CPC, with conflicting sources:
  - SuperAds tracker: India CPM averaged about US$1.98 (about ₹165) over Dec 2024 to Nov 2025, with a low of $1.14 in Nov 2025. India median CPC was about $0.11 (about ₹9), ranging from $0.21 (Aug 2025) to $0.025 (Feb 2026). — [SuperAds CPM India](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india), [SuperAds CPC India](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/india/brand-awareness)
  - Vaizle (2025): India average CPC about ₹34 ($0.41), against $1.67 in the US. — [Vaizle](https://insights.vaizle.com/facebook-ads-cost/)
  - OwlClaw (agency client data, 2025 standards): CPC ₹8 to ₹25, CPM ₹50 to ₹120, Instagram Feed CPC ₹14 to ₹28. — [OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/meta-ads-benchmarks-india)
  - Smartads.in (agency): India Meta CPM about ₹80 to ₹250 depending on audience. — [Smartads](https://smartads.in/services/digital/social-media-marketing-digital-advertising)
- Meta A/B tests (WhatsApp Business success stories, as tabulated by Spur, Oct 2026):
  - Nutryfit (India and Gulf): optimising for leads instead of conversations gave 12% lower cost per lead (test ran 1 to 18 Apr 2025).
  - Learnwise (India): WhatsApp Flows for qualification gave 4x qualified leads and 31% lower cost per qualified lead (self-reported).
  - Purchase optimisation instead of conversation optimisation elsewhere: Confer On (Indonesia) had 79% lower cost per purchase; VIP Leather Craft (Indonesia) 21% lower; Thành Vinh (Malaysia) 40% lower.

  — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- Meta experiment, H2 2024, 734,000 businesses, three weeks, global: ads with more than one message destination (for example WhatsApp plus Instagram DM plus Messenger) had a 9.5% lower median cost per message than WhatsApp-only ads. — [Spur citing Meta's click-to-message page](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- For a ₹3,599+ personalised gift (a considered purchase, but an emotional one with a strong visual hook), a realistic planning range is about ₹20 to ₹60 per conversation started. This is an estimate that sits between GRWW's clothing figures and its wedding-studio figures. Cheap "free AI preview" chats may land at the low end, but with more tyre-kickers.
- Cost per order is what matters. At ₹30 per chat and 5% of chats converting to orders, cost per order is about ₹600, roughly 17% of a ₹3,599 single. That is an illustrative estimate, and the owner should replace it with their own chat-to-order rate.

### Gaps
- No India gifting, D2C-personalised or figurine-specific CTWA benchmark was found.
- No India CTR benchmark for Reels CTWA beyond GRWW's two data points.
- USD figures were converted at about ₹83 to ₹84 per $ (approximate).

## Q2. WhatsApp Business costs: app vs API with CTWA, free entry point window, per-message pricing, India marketing rates

### Takeaway
The free WhatsApp Business app can be linked to a Facebook Page or Instagram account and used as a CTWA destination. Practitioners say most small businesses should start there. Meta's per-message fees apply only to the WhatsApp Business Platform (API). Meta's official pricing page, as of Oct 2026, says:

- The free entry point window after a CTWA chat is now "up to 7 days", where older guides say 72 hours.
- Pricing has been per delivered message since 1 Jul 2025.
- From 1 Oct 2026, service replies beyond 1,000 a month per number, and utility messages inside the 24-hour window, are charged.

India marketing templates are about ₹0.86 per message plus 18% GST.

### Cited Findings
- **App with CTWA:** a free WhatsApp Business app number linked to your Facebook Page "works fine", and most small businesses should start there. The API is needed once volume or automation grows. — [Shreyas Bagal, 17 Sep 2026](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide); see also [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/create-click-to-whatsapp-ads/) (the app is fine for testing and low volume; the API is for scale and automation)
- **Linking requirements:** admin access to the Facebook Page; the WhatsApp number must not be linked to another Page; the Instagram account connected to the same Page for Instagram placements; an ad account with a valid payment method and no restrictions. Business Manager ownership mismatches are the most common setup failure. — [Clickatell help center](https://www.clickatell.com/help-center/whatsapp/marketing-commerce/how-do-you-set-up-your-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/), [YCloud](https://helpdocs.ycloud.com/help-center/ctwa-click-to-whatsapp-ad/meta-ctwa-ads/create-click-to-whatsapp-ad-ctwa)
- **In-app ads:** WhatsApp announced in June 2023 that Business app users would be able to create Facebook and Instagram CTWA ads from inside the app, with no Facebook account (email plus payment only). I found no source confirming current availability in India. — [Social Samosa, Jun 2023](https://www.socialsamosa.com/2023/06/whatsapp-business-new-features-personalized-messages)
- **Pricing model:** "Since July 1, 2025," Meta charges "on a per-message basis" for business-to-user messages. Conversation-based pricing is deprecated. Meta charges only on delivery, and rates vary by category and recipient market. — [Meta developer docs, WhatsApp pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **Free entry point (FEP) window:**
  - Meta's current docs: when a user messages through a CTWA ad (Android or iOS) and the business replies within the customer service window, an FEP window opens that "may remain open for up to 7 days". Meta Business Agent messages are still charged inside it. — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
  - Spur says this changed from 72 hours to "up to 7 days" in a docs update dated 28 Sep 2026. — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
  - Conflict: a guide dated 17 Sep 2026 still says 72 hours. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)

  Meta's own page should win.
- **Customer service window:** 24 hours from the user's last message or call, reset by each new one. Non-template messages can only be sent while it is open. — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **Changes from 1 Oct 2026:**
  - Service messages were free from 1 Nov 2024. Now each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month (no rollover), and beyond that they are charged.
  - Utility messages inside an open customer service window, free since 1 Jul 2025, are now charged.
  - Businesses with no payment method on file by 30 Sep 2026 may have service messages stop being delivered.

  — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **India-specific (Meta docs):**
  - India marketing rates rose on 1 Jan 2026.
  - INR billing launched 1 Jan 2026 for eligible customers. Messaging accounts must move to INR by 31 Dec 2026, or messages stop on 1 Jan 2027.
  - India is not among the markets with rate changes on 1 Oct 2026.
  - Meta changes prices only on the first day of a quarter.

  — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **India rates (secondhand, from BSP and vendor blogs; the rate card CSV was not fetched):**
  - Marketing template ₹0.8631 (about $0.0118) per message; it was ₹0.7846 before 1 Jan 2026.
  - Utility and authentication ₹0.115 per message.
  - All excluding 18% GST.

  — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks), [DEV Community](https://dev.to/preciousky_45d956626d31c3/whatsapp-business-platform-pricing-in-rupees-and-the-two-things-that-change-on-1-october-2026-4ko0), [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/)
- **Earlier USD history:** TechRadar reported India marketing rates rising from $0.0099 to $0.0107 (+8%) and utility rates falling from $0.0042 to $0.0014 (−67%). — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/whatsapp-business-is-changing-messgaing-rate-to-cut-down-on-spam)

### Inferences
- **For Yours3D today:** WhatsApp costs on the Business app are effectively ₹0. This assumes the app carries no per-message fees, which comes from my background knowledge and is consistent with the pricing docs applying only to the Platform.
- **If they move to the API** (for a chatbot that sends AI previews automatically, or to report purchases to Meta via CAPI, see Q4), the 7-day FEP makes follow-ups to ad-driven leads free inside that window. Re-engagement broadcasts later cost about ₹0.86 + GST, roughly ₹1.02 each. 1,000 marketing messages would cost about ₹1,020 (my arithmetic).
- **App limits that matter:** CAPI purchase reporting needs the Platform (Q4), and the app runs on a limited number of devices. Those are the main reasons to upgrade, not price.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Meta's official India rate card CSV or PDF directly, so ₹0.8631 is confirmed only through secondhand sources.
- I could not confirm whether in-app "Advertise" from the WhatsApp Business app is live in India in Oct 2026.
- A tyntec guide (images dated about 2023) says "the Messages objective is unavailable when targeting India and selected EU countries." — [tyntec](https://www.tyntec.com/helpcenter/docs/internal/create-click-to-whatsapp-ads-on-facebook-and-instagram/). This looks outdated or specific to the old Messages objective: 2026 Indian guides set up CTWA via the Engagement, Leads or Sales objective with WhatsApp as the conversion location. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide). Not verified against Meta.

## Q3. Campaign setup: objectives, Advantage+, learning phase, budgets, audiences, placements, creative, partnership ads, boost vs Ads Manager

### Takeaway
Use Ads Manager, not the Instagram Boost button. Build one campaign with:

- the Engagement (or Leads, or Sales) objective, conversion location WhatsApp (optionally also Instagram DM and Messenger)
- one consolidated ad set with Advantage+ audience
- Reels and Stories placements included
- 3 to 6 vertical creatives (making-of Reels plus testimonials)

Meta's ad-set learning phase needs about 50 optimisation events in a rolling 7 days. At ₹500 to ₹2,000 a day that is reachable when optimising for conversations, but not for purchases. Partnership ads using real customers' or creators' posts are Meta-backed and worth testing.

### Cited Findings
- **Objective setup (2026 India practitioner):** in Ads Manager choose Engagement (or Leads) and set the ad set's conversion location to WhatsApp. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide). Older guides say Engagement too. — [Clickatell](https://www.clickatell.com/help-center/whatsapp/marketing-commerce/how-do-you-set-up-your-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)
- **Meta's CTWA product page** frames these ads as supporting "lead generation, sales and marketing". It lists Meta pixel, Conversions API and Offline Conversions for measurement, and gives no figures. — [WhatsApp Business, ads that click to WhatsApp](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)
- **Optimising for leads or purchases instead of conversations** cut cost per result by 12% to 79% in Meta's A/B tests (Nutryfit India: −12% cost per lead). — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- **Advantage+:**
  - Meta renamed Advantage+ Shopping campaigns to Advantage+ Sales campaigns around Feb 2025. The change was more than a rename: it now allows multiple ad sets, and the scope covers sales, leads and app installs. — [Embryo](https://embryo.com/?p=91295), [Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-advantage-plus-shopping-campaigns/)
  - Old ASC can no longer be created or updated on Marketing API v24. — [Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-advantage-plus-shopping-campaigns/)
  - These are third-party sources, and I could not fetch Meta's own announcement (Jon Loomer page returned 403).
- **Learning phase:**
  - About 50 optimisation events per ad set within a rolling 7-day window. The optimisation event is whatever was chosen (conversation, lead, purchase). — [Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-learning-phase/), [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/learning-phase-optimization)
  - Too little budget leaves the ad set in "Learning Limited". — [AdStellar](https://www.adstellar.ai/blog/meta-advertising-learning-phase-issues)
  - Budget changes above about 20%, and edits to targeting, bid or optimisation event, restart learning. — [AdMake](https://admakeai.com/blog/facebook-ads-learning-phase-explained), [Niblin](https://niblin.com/blog/meta-ads-learning-phase)
  - Consolidating into fewer ad sets is the usual fix. — [AdsX](https://www.adsx.com/blog/meta-ads-learning-phase-exit-tactics), [Deepsolv](https://deepsolv.ai/feed/meta-ads-learning-phase)
  - Rule of thumb for daily budget: (target cost per result × 50) ÷ 7. — [Grow with Sakib](https://growwithsakib.com/meta-ads-learning-phase/)
  - Meta's own Help Center page ("About the learning phase") could not be fetched, so these are secondhand.
- **Starting budget:** ₹300 to ₹500 a day is enough for a single-location business. Use Advantage+ audience (with a tight radius for local businesses), run all day for two weeks, and wait about a week before judging. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)
- **Creative (GRWW, Oct 2026):**
  - 3 to 6 creatives per ad set, including at least one vertical video.
  - Show real work in the first seconds.
  - A specific offer beats a generic "contact us".
  - A distinct pre-filled message per campaign.
  - Test Hindi, regional language or Hinglish copy.
  - Refresh creatives about monthly or when costs rise.
  - Reels often had the highest CTR.

  — [GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)
- **Partnership ads:**
  - Meta's own data reports 19% lower CPA and 13% higher CTR against business-as-usual ads. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance)
  - Testimonial overlays gave 7.5% higher off-site conversions and 9.6% higher off-site CTR. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-unveils-new-features-streamline-creator-partnerships)
  - The Partnership Ads Hub "All" tab collects Instagram UGC and affiliate posts that tag or mention the brand, so the advertiser can pick which to amplify. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-unveils-new-features-streamline-creator-partnerships)
  - Vendor case studies report 20% to 50% lower CPA (vendor-biased). — [Aspire](https://www.aspire.io/blog/partnership-ads-2025-hack-to-slash-paid-ad-costs), [Influee](https://influee.co/gb/content-hub/babylovegrow-case-study)
- **Boost vs Ads Manager:**
  - Boost turns an existing post or Reel into an ad in about 30 seconds but gives less targeting, reporting and conversion tracking. It cannot retarget website visitors, and it doesn't show the follower vs non-follower split. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/boost-instagram-posts/), [Conbersa](https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/instagram-boosted-posts-vs-ads-manager), [Clever Clicks](https://clever-clicks.co.uk/blog/meta-ads-manager-vs-boosted-posts-whats-the-difference/)
  - One UK guide says boosting may be fine under £50 a campaign. — [Digital Culture Network](https://digitalculturenetwork.org.uk/knowledge/whats-the-difference-between-boosted-posts-and-ad-campaigns-on-facebook-and-instagram/)
  - An ASU brand guide says neither is strictly better. — [ASU](https://brandguide.asu.edu/index.php/execution-guidelines/social-media/paid)
  - The Indian CTWA practitioner lists "using the Boost button instead of Ads Manager" as a pitfall. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)

### Inferences
- **Learning phase arithmetic:**
  - At ₹500/day and ₹30 per conversation, that is about 117 conversations a week, which exits learning comfortably when optimising for conversations.
  - Optimising for purchases at an illustrative ₹600 per purchase needs 50 × 600 ÷ 7, about ₹4,300/day for one ad set, above the ₹2,000 ceiling.
  - So: start on conversations (or leads), and switch to purchase optimisation only once CAPI is sending purchases and the budget can support it.
- **Use boosting only** to amplify a top-performing organic Reel for reach. Run lead-gen CTWA in Ads Manager, where you can choose the optimisation event, add multiple creatives and see placement breakdowns.
- **Partnership ads suit Yours3D's testimonial Reels:** customers post the figurine unboxing, tag the brand, and grant partnership permission.

### Gaps
- Meta's exact minimum daily budget in INR for an engagement or messaging ad set wasn't found, and it varies by currency and optimisation event.
- No Reddit threads (r/FacebookAds, r/IndiaBusiness) surfaced through search on boost vs Ads Manager or on CTWA. The consensus above comes from blogs and practitioners.
- Meta's official India CTWA case studies with rupee figures were not found. The WhatsApp success-stories page lists Nutryfit and others without numbers. — [WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)

## Q4. Pitfalls and measurement: junk chats, broad audiences, tracking and CAPI for business messaging, account bans, payments, GST

### Takeaway
The biggest practical risks:

- treating cheap chats as success when many are "price?" or "hi" tyre-kickers
- slow replies
- no source tracking
- bans and payment failures (risk is highest on new accounts)

Measurement is solved properly only by CAPI for Business Messaging, which needs the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), not the app. Indian advertisers pay 18% GST on Meta ads.

### Cited Findings
- **Chats aren't qualified leads.** Many people only want a price; discounts attract bargain hunters; Ads Manager counts conversations, not bookings; conversation counts may mix WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram; slow replies waste spend. — [GRWW](https://grww.tech/blog/click-whatsapp-ads-get-enquiries-at-lowest-cost)
- **A generic "Hi" pre-filled message** gives no context and hides which ad drove the chat. Practitioner pitfall list:
  - launching before the greeting and quick replies are ready
  - mixing ad leads with personal chats on one number
  - editing the ad set daily
  - nobody watching WhatsApp during ad hours
  - judging by cost per chat instead of bookings
  - staying on one app number as volume grows

  Fix: label chats hot, warm or junk and send outcomes back to Meta. — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)
- **Qualification flows** raised the qualified share from 22% to 37% in the House Hunt case. — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- **CAPI for Business Messaging:**
  - A CTWA click yields a `ctwa_clid` ("unique per click"), exposed to the business when the conversation came from a CTWA ad. The business stores it and sends conversion events (`action_source: business_messaging`, `messaging_channel: whatsapp`, with value and currency) to a dataset.
  - It requires a WhatsApp Business Platform integration; Meta recommends the Cloud API.
  - The On-Premises client expired 23 Oct 2025.
  - Advanced Access requires 1,500 successful Marketing API calls with less than 10% errors in 15 days.

  — [Meta developer docs, CAPI for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
  - Vendors (Wati, Connectly, Woztell, Datahash) offer turnkey CTWA CAPI. Connectly notes rejections such as "Invalid Ctwa Clid", and Purchase needs currency and value. — [Connectly](https://docs.connectly.ai/analytics/capi-conversion-reporting-endpoint), [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/track-ctwa-conversions-capi/)
  - A vendor claims purchase optimisation for CTWA needs about 10 events in 7 days. This is unverified in Meta docs. — [Cliengo](https://guiawabusiness.cliengo.com/conversions-api-whatsapp-capi)
- **Account and payment risk:**
  - Meta may migrate Indian accounts to prepaid payment (RBI-related), which pauses campaigns temporarily. — [Velocity](https://blog.velocity.in/facebook-ads-blocked-it-could-be-because-of-the-rbis-new-guidelines/)
  - Repeated payment failures, cardholder-name mismatches and frequent card changes are cited triggers for disabled accounts. — [Pixelscan](https://pixelscan.net/blog/how-to-recover-your-disabled-instagram-ad-account/), [Shopify community](https://community.shopify.com/t/meta-suspended-payment-method-we-noticed-some-unusual-activity-so-weve-disabled-your-ad-account/356293)
  - Indian agency Blink Digital sued Meta in the Bombay High Court (reported claim ₹1,000 crore) over four disabled Business Manager accounts. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=573487)
- **GST:**
  - Digital advertising in India is taxed at 18%, a rate that survived the 2025 GST rationalisation.
  - If the invoice comes from Meta's Indian entity with a GSTIN and GST charged, it is a normal forward charge.
  - If billed by Meta Platforms Ireland with no GST, the buyer self-assesses 18% IGST under reverse charge (GSTR-3B 3.1(d)) and claims input tax credit.
  - GST-registered businesses can claim input tax credit.
  - These are tax-advisory blogs, so the owner should confirm with a CA.

  — [Taxgarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-advertising-services-google-meta-ads-rcm-india-2026), [TaxAJ](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=519)

### Inferences
- **Junk-chat filter:** Yours3D's "free AI preview" offer is a strong hook, but it will attract curiosity chats. Use:
  - a pre-filled message that asks for the photo and occasion (for example "Hi, I want a figurine preview. Occasion: ___")
  - an instant greeting quoting "Singles from ₹3,599"
  - a quick-reply price card

  Then track chat → preview sent → order in a sheet with per-ad pre-filled tags.
- **Tracking:** iOS ATT matters less for CTWA, because the conversion happens inside WhatsApp rather than on a website. This is inference, and the real gap is that Meta never sees orders without CAPI.
- **Ban and payment safety:** use a card in the business owner's name matching the ad account (or prepaid UPI/netbanking funding), keep two admins on the Business Manager, and avoid sudden big budget jumps on a brand-new account (inference).

### Gaps
- No primary Meta India billing page was fetched. UPI and prepaid availability and RBI e-mandate interaction are not confirmed from Meta.
- No first-hand Reddit reports on CTWA bot or spam rates were found. "Hi"-spam frequency is anecdotal.
- Ad policy specifics for personalised products made from customer photos (for example likeness or celebrity images) were not researched.

## Q5. Starter plan practitioners recommend for ₹500 to ₹2,000 a day

### Takeaway
Practitioners recommend:

1. One Ads Manager campaign: Engagement or Leads objective, conversion location WhatsApp.
2. One ad set: Advantage+ audience, all-India or the top metros, automatic placements with Reels and Stories.
3. 3 to 6 vertical creatives.
4. Run ₹300 to ₹500+ a day untouched for 7 to 14 days.
5. Judge on cost per qualified chat and per order.
6. Scale by 20% or less at a time.
7. Add CAPI and purchase optimisation once on the API.

### Cited Findings
- **Steps** (17 Sep 2026):
  1. Link WhatsApp to the Page in Business Suite.
  2. Choose Engagement or Leads with conversion location WhatsApp.
  3. Run ₹300 to ₹500 a day all day for two weeks.
  4. Use Advantage+ audience.
  5. Make a 15 to 30 second vertical video.
  6. Write a specific pre-filled message, greeting and quick replies before launch.
  7. Test-tap your own ad.
  8. Wait about a week before judging.

  — [Shreyas Bagal](https://shreyasbagal.in/click-to-whatsapp-ads-guide)
