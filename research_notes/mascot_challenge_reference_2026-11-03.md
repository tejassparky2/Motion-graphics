# Reference: "Is Grim AI?" mascot challenge video (owner upload, 3 Nov 2026)

Owner: "Can you make videos like this? Cute affordable and informative". The file is not committed (public repo).

## What it is
- 2:06, landscape 960x540. Another creator's mascot ("Grim", a chibi grim reaper): never copy the character, its
  name or its look; make our own.
- An off-screen owner talks to the mascot ("Grim, a lot of people in the comments think you're AI"); the mascot
  answers in tiny squeaky lines shown as small red text over its head, not as captions. Captions at the bottom.
- Mascot: big head, tiny body, huge glossy yellow eyes, many expressions (happy, crying, angry, eyes shut), lots of
  bounce. Cut out and placed on real photos: interrogation room, boat on a lake, strawberry field, gym floor.
- Game structure: real YouTube comments flying in as the hook; an "AI suss-o-meter" bar (0% -> 21% -> 59% -> 82% ->
  100%); three glowing portal gates, one per challenge (water, strawberry, dance).
- Each challenge is a joke built on an AI meme: AI "drinks a lot of water" (data-centre cooling; the gag drains the
  lake to cracked mud), AI "can't count the r's in strawberry", AI "can't dance". Facts are thin.
- Ending: the meter maxes out, the mascot cries ("u won't love me anymore"), then real hands pick it up: "I love
  you no matter what. Now let's go get candy."

## What carries over to Interestingly Strange (vertical, 40-50 s)
- An original mascot of our own, a meter that rises with each test, three quick tests, a twist, a warm or funny end.
- Make each test teach one real, checked fact (the reference's facts are memes), and end on our either/or question.
- Line 1 stays a character speaking to the mascot (fits "Line 1 = a character asking the impossible question").
- Photo backgrounds need a licence (free stock with commercial use) or our own painted scenes; real hands need the
  owner's own footage.

# Reference 2: "Grim, try and kill this tardigrade" (owner upload, 3 Nov 2026: "1 more reference")
- 0:42, vertical 720x1280: our Shorts format. The same mascot and an off-screen owner, on ONE real photo (a wooden
  floor by a sofa) for the whole video: cheap to make.
- Line 1 is the owner's demand ("Grim, try and kill this tardigrade"); the mascot answers in small red text ("but y
  tho?", "ok fine", "it so gross", "sorry").
- Each attempt is a prop gag that carries one real fact: scythe -> the tardigrade curls into a "tun" when stressed;
  flamethrower -> survives immense heat; radiation gun -> survives radiation "enough to kill a human"; it can even
  survive outer space; a little water and it's back to normal.
- Real hands twice (pointing at the start, blocking the radiation gun: "watch where ur pointing that").
- Twist: "well then I guess it would die-" as it's back in normal form, a slash flash, "OH MY GOD". Cut.
- Simple animation: pose swaps, sticker-like cut-outs, small shakes. Caption font is comic-style, centred mid-frame.
