## 12. The blind spot — `videos/blind_spot.py`
- The optic disc (where the optic nerve and blood vessels leave the eye) has no photoreceptors, so every eye has a
  blind spot, about 15 degrees from the centre of vision, roughly 6 x 8 degrees across.
- The brain fills it in from the surrounding picture ("perceptual filling-in"); with both eyes open, each eye covers
  the other's blind spot. Raman & Sarkar, "Predictive Coding: A Possible Explanation of Filling-In at the Blind
  Spot", PLoS One 2016 (PMC4784844): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4784844/
- Test on screen: cross at the left, dot at the right, full card width (about 5 cm apart on a phone, so the dot
  vanishes at roughly 20 cm). Close the LEFT eye, stare at the cross with the RIGHT eye: the dot falls on the right
  eye's blind spot (on the temporal, i.e. right, side of its view).

## 13. Taller in the morning — `videos/morning_height.py`
- The 23 intervertebral discs lose water under the day's load and soak it back up lying down; people are tallest
  just after waking. Typical loss over a day: about 1 cm (1-2 cm reported). Script: "about one centimetre".
- In microgravity the discs aren't loaded: astronauts can grow about 3% taller (NASA; NASA/TM-20250002737, "Concern
  of Intervertebral Disc Damage": https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20250002737/downloads/20250002737.pdf ;
  ScienceDaily 2016, "Space travel affects spine of astronauts":
  https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2016/10/161025135954.htm).

## 14. The stomach's cleaning wave — `videos/stomach_clean.py`
- Between meals the migrating motor complex (MMC) recurs about every 90-120 minutes; its strongest phase sweeps from
  the stomach through the small intestine, clearing leftover food, secretions and bacteria ("interdigestive
  housekeeper", Szurszewski). Linked to the hormone motilin; eating interrupts it.
  Wikipedia, "Migrating motor complex": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migrating_motor_complex ; Mondal et al.,
  "The role of the vagus nerve in the migrating motor complex...", PMC3665597:
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3665597/
- Growling (borborygmi) is gas and fluid pushed along. Hunger is real too, so the script says "It's also us,
  cleaning" rather than "it doesn't mean hunger".
