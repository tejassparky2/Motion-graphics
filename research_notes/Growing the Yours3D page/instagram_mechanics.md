Research date: 27 October 2026. Every claim is dated and labelled **[Official]** (Instagram/Meta/Mosseri statement), **[Measured]** (third-party dataset with sample size), **[Vendor/opinion]** (tool-maker blog, agency, creator opinion; weakest), or **[Older]** (pre-2025). ## KQ1. What Instagram officially says about Reels ranking (2025–2026) and what changed

### Takeaway
Instagram's only explicit public weighting (22 Jan 2025, Mosseri) is: the three signals that matter most are **average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**, with sends slightly more important for non-followers ("unconnected" reach) and likes slightly more for followers. The 2025–26 changes that matter for a small page are: 3-minute Reels (Jan 2025), unified **Views** metric (Apr 2025), **Retention chart + Skip Rate** insights (Aug 2025), **Your Algorithm** user controls (Dec 2025 → platform-wide Jun 2026), and an **originality rule** (Apr 2026) that removes accounts that mostly repost others' content from recommendations to non-followers. Trial Reels (tested May 2024, broadly available 2025) let you show a Reel to non-followers first.

### Cited Findings

**Ranking signals (official)**
- [Official, 22 Jan 2025] Mosseri's first video of a 2025 "how ranking works" series: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He told creators to pay "close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Comments were not mentioned. — [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- [Official, undated, via Buffer's 2026 guide] Instagram's stated Reels ranking inputs: the viewer's recent activity (likes, saves, reshares, comments), interaction history with the poster, information about the Reel (visuals, audio, watch time, early engagement) and poster signals (follower count, engagement levels). Explore weights "post popularity" (likes, comments, shares, saves and how quickly they arrive) more than Feed or Stories do. — [Buffer, "How the Instagram Algorithm Works" (2026 guide)](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/), citing [Instagram "Instagram ranking explained"](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained/)
- [Official, via Buffer] Mosseri (ranking series, 2026, undated reel): "Too often creators look at how many views a post got or how many likes a reel got, but not at the [engagement] rate." Also: small public accounts' posts are "auditioned" with non-followers and pushed further if they perform well; a growing share of daily impressions goes to posts under 24 hours old (no figures given); ranking models have become "larger and larger." — [Buffer 2026 guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)
- [Official, via Buffer] Instagram says hashtags do not help reach (Mosseri reel, undated), Reels with third-party-app logos (e.g. TikTok watermarks) are pushed down, and Stories are "not typically the best way to reach new people." Buffer also cites a @creators Threads post saying the hashtag cap is now five. — [Buffer 2026 guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)
- [Official, undated] Instagram's creators site: "Reels makes up over 50% of all time spent on Instagram." "Each part of the Instagram app uses its own algorithm." The page itself lists no ranking signals. — [creators.instagram.com, Algorithms and ranking](https://creators.instagram.com/grow/algorithms-and-ranking?locale=en_GB)
- [Official, via Lia Haberman, 14 Oct 2025] Mosseri said Reels and DMs drove most of Instagram's growth in recent years, and explained carousel success by "more interactions per post, which on average means more reach," plus the algorithm resurfacing a carousel to followers who swiped past it ("a second chance after it's posted"). — [ICYMI by Lia Haberman, 14 Oct 2025](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)

**What changed, with dates**
- [Official via press, 21 Jan 2025] Reels length limit raised from 90 s to 3 minutes, announced by Mosseri on Threads, citing creator feedback that "90 seconds was just too short"; profile grid moved to rectangles the same day. — [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-officially-expands-reels-length-to-3-minutes/737766/); [Business Today, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)
- [Official via vendors, effective 21 Apr 2025] Meta replaced "Plays" and "Impressions" with one **Views** metric across posts, Reels, Stories and ads; repeat views by the same person count. Sources differ on the minimum (ListenFirst: 1 ms; Social Media Today: "1-second view"); the old 3-second threshold belonged to the retired Video Views metric. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/instagram-views-metrics-changes); [ListenFirst](https://www.listenfirstmedia.com/ig-views-metric/); [Social Media Today infographic](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/how-social-platforms-measure-content-views-infographic/723912/)
- [Official via Indian press, 25 Aug 2025] Instagram added a per-Reel **Retention chart** and **Skip Rate** (share of viewers who leave within the first 3 seconds; replaces "View Rate", which measured those who watched past 3 s). Instagram: "With Retention, you'll always see a downward slope, but the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience is." and "Retention shows you the moments in your Reel that keep or lose your viewers' interest." — [Social Samosa, 25 Aug 2025](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/); [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/instagram-introduces-skip-rate-new-metric-to-make-brandcreator-conversations-tougher-146829.html)
- [Official, 10 Dec 2025; updated 15 Apr 2026 and 10 Jun 2026] **Your Algorithm**: AI summary of the topics driving a user's recommendations, which the user can edit ("Type in the topics you want to see more or less of"). Started on Reels (US), extended to Explore (globally in English, Apr 2026) and to Feed (Jun 2026). Feed posts recommended from an interest now carry a label such as "From Running"; "It's all one system." No ranking signals disclosed; no creator guidance. — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control)
- [Official via press, ~30 Apr 2026] **Originality rule**: Mosseri: "If most of what you post to Instagram is someone else's content, your account is no longer going to be recommendable." Evaluated on a rolling 30-day window; previously Reels-only, now also photos and carousels. Followers still see posts; the account loses Explore/suggested/non-follower recommendations. Watermarks, slight crops or caption credits do not count as "meaningful transformation"; commentary, remixing, reinterpretation or original framing do. Near-identical reposts get replaced in recommendations by the original and the original creator is notified; reposts carry a credit label. — [Planoly, updated 5 May 2026](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy); [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026 (403 to fetch; headline only)](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/). Note: a 2025-era Instagram statement used a different threshold ("10 or more times in the last 30 days"); the 2026 wording is "most of what you post". Which cutoff now applies is unclear from public sources.
- [Older, Jan 2025 context] Mosseri's Jan 2025 year-ahead video said Instagram would fine-tune ranking to prioritise "creative and original posts over reposts or low-quality material." — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/how-instagram-plans-to-lead-the-social-media-landscape-by-2025-53450.htm)
- [Official via press, 31 May 2024 = Older] **Trial Reels** first reported as a test: a Reel shown to non-followers rather than followers, with insights (plays, likes, comments, shares) after which the creator can share to followers or archive; evolved from "Experiment mode". Meta then: "nothing to share at the moment." — [TechCrunch, 31 May 2024](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2787261)
- [Vendor, 2025–26] Vendor guides state Trial Reels launched late 2024, expanded to all creators with 1,000+ followers by mid-2025; followers can still see a trial via DM or on the same audio/location page; metrics appear ~24 h after posting; an optional auto-share to followers can trigger after 72 hours, with no published threshold. — [Publer](https://publer.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-guide/); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/). Treat the 1,000-follower eligibility and 72-hour details as vendor-reported, not official.
- [Vendor] Several 2026 blogs claim "DM sends weigh 3–5× likes" or a "July 2026 skip-rate ranking update"; a debunking table in the same search notes Mosseri only ever said "slightly more important," no multiplier was ever published, and no Instagram post backs the July 2026 claim. — [reeldrop.io tracker](https://www.reeldrop.io/learn/instagram-algorithm); [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/). Treat such multipliers as unsupported.

**Instagram SEO**
- [Vendor, citing Mosseri] Semrush, citing Mosseri, says search text is the most important factor for Instagram search, matched against usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and locations, then personalised by activity. A Kontentino Q&A claims Mosseri described (July 2026) a search system reading captions and comments as text plus embedding models over photos/video; this could not be verified against a primary source. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/instagram-seo/); [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/)
- [Vendor, 2025] Public Instagram content from professional accounts can appear in Google; captions, alt text and the first caption line act like search headlines; brands can opt out via a "Search Engine Indexing" setting; personal/private accounts excluded. — [WERSM](https://wersm.com/?p=114408); [Pulse Advertising](https://www.pulse-advertising.com/?p=21220). Could not verify against Instagram's help centre (login-walled).

### Inferences
- For a small account, the official guidance reduces to three actions: maximise average watch time (hook + no dead air), make Reels worth forwarding to a friend (sends per reach is the stated non-follower lever), and keep original creation above 50% of posts in any 30-day window so recommendations are not switched off.
- Hashtags and third-party watermarks are officially neutral-to-negative; caption keywords and on-screen text are the only official search levers.
- "Views" now counts repeat plays, so views inflate relative to pre-April-2025 numbers; "reach" (unique accounts) is the honest denominator for likes-per-reach and sends-per-reach, which is why Mosseri frames the signals as rates.

### Gaps
- No official document lists the ranking signals with weights; the Jan 2025 statement is the only weighting language, and even it is qualitative ("slightly").
- Could not confirm from Instagram itself the Trial Reels follower-count eligibility, the 72-hour auto-share rule, or any Trial-Reel changes in 2026.
- The precise 2026 originality threshold ("most" vs "10 in 30 days") is not resolved by public sources.
- No official statement on "theme consistency" of an account was found; the only related signals are Your Algorithm topic labels and Explore's interest matching (inference, not official).

## KQ2. Benchmark studies 2025–2026: reach, engagement, saves, posting frequency, small-account growth

### Takeaway
Across the big datasets, Instagram engagement per follower is low and falling (Socialinsider: 0.45–0.52% depending on format and quarter; Rival IQ per-follower median reported as ~0.30–0.36%), **Reels give the highest reach for accounts under ~50K followers** (Socialinsider H1 2026: 9.78% reach rate for 1–5K, 7.55% for 5–10K), **carousels edge Reels on engagement**, and small accounts (<10K) are the one group whose engagement grew in 2025 (Metricool). Posting-frequency data conflicts: more posts/week correlates with faster follower growth (Metricool/HypeAuditor) but with lower reach rate per post (Shield Index).

### Cited Findings

**Socialinsider (business accounts 1K–1M followers)**
- [Measured, 20 Feb 2026; 67.5M organic posts, ~580K accounts] Annual engagement rate (likes+comments ÷ followers): Reels 0.52% (2025) vs 0.50% (2024); carousels 0.55% both years; images 0.37% (2025) vs 0.45%, i.e. images −17% YoY; overall 0.48%, down 24% YoY. Quarterly (labelled Q3 2026 on the page): Reels 0.48%, carousel 0.50%, images 0.35%, platform 0.45%. Average posts per month: Reels 8 (2025, up from 6), carousels 5, images 7. Audience growth rate 2025 vs 2024: 1–5K accounts 22% (was 38%); 5–10K 20.3% (was 35%); 10–50K 17.2%. Average saves per post for 1–5K: 1 (all formats); 5–10K: 2 Reels/2 carousel/1 image. Average views per post for 1–5K: Reels 580, carousel 993, image 417. Note the page's own caveat that some "2026" labels hold 2025 data. — [Socialinsider Instagram Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmarks/)
- [Measured, published 24 Jun 2026, updated 21 Oct 2026; 9.77M Reels, 295K profiles, Jan–Jun 2026] Reels reach rate (reach ÷ followers): 1–5K 9.78%, 5–10K 7.55%, 10–50K 7.10%, 50–100K 5.60%, 100K–1M 5.00%; carousels overtake Reels on reach above 50K (5.85% and 6.00%). Reach rate by Reel length: <30 s 5.20%, **30–60 s 5.60% (highest)**, 60–90 s 5.30%, 90–120 s 4.30%, >120 s 3.50%. Skip rate (swiped away within 3 s ÷ plays): 65.5% for 1–5K and 65.3% for 5–10K, the highest of any size. Reels per month: 8 (1–5K), 10 (5–10K). Saves/shares excluded as private metrics. — [Socialinsider Reels Statistics 2026](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)
- [Measured, Q2 2026 page] Reels engagement 0.48% in Q2 2026 (from 0.50% in Q1), carousels 0.50% (from 0.52%), images 0.33% (from 0.35%). — [Socialinsider Reels engagement](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-engagement/)

**Metricool / HypeAuditor**
- [Measured, press release 14 Oct 2025; 700M posts, 28M influencer accounts, Jan–Jun 2025, medians] Reels lead in reach across all follower tiers ("the most effective path to new audiences"); carousels gain on likes/comments as accounts grow, especially at 500K+. Posting 2–3 times a week ≈ 19% average growth; 10+ posts a week ≈ 79% growth "most likely" (not tied to a follower tier). Prime posting window 8 p.m. local; top days Wednesday and Friday. Recommends Trial Reels "to refine hooks and formats". — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/)
- [Measured, via Lia Haberman 14 Oct 2025] Same study: 10K–50K accounts get highest median reach from Reels (47% of their posts are Reels); 50K–500K: carousels 9.3K median reach vs Reels 8K. — [ICYMI](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)
- [Measured, Sept 2025; 5.7M videos, ~600K accounts] Metricool "State of Short-Form Video 2025": Instagram Reels posts +34% and posting accounts +26% YoY, but reach −22%, views −59%, engagement −30%. **Small accounts (<10K followers) were the exception: likes +29%, interactions +22%, engagement +30%.** — [Influencer Marketing Hub summary](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-dominates-engagement-youtube-shorts-falter/); [press release](https://socialmediatoday.com/press-release/20250908-metricool-2025-study-unveils-70-surge-in-short-form-video-as-tiktok-conti)
- [Measured, 2025] Metricool's 2025 Social Media Study: Reels had the highest engagement rate of any video format, 5.84%, for accounts with 2K–50K followers (formula not stated in the excerpt). — [Metricool Reels length article](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length)

**Buffer**
- [Measured, 23 Oct 2024 = Older; 4M+ posts, Jan 2022–Oct 2024] Reels get 1.36× (36%) more reach than carousels and 2.25× more than single images; carousels get 1.12× (12%) more engagement than Reels and 2.14× more than images. No account-size breakdown. — [Buffer reach/engagement analysis](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/)
- [Measured, 2026 via Buffer guide] Consistent posters (100K+ users) get nearly 5× engagement per post; accounts posting 20+ weeks do best. Buffer's 2026 engagement report: posts where the account replied to comments got 21% more engagement (correlation). Best times from 9.6M posts: Thu 9 a.m., Wed 12 p.m., Wed 6 p.m. (time zone = poster's local). — [Buffer 2026 guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)

**Rival IQ, Hootsuite, Later**
- [Measured, 25 Feb 2025; 4M+ posts, 9B interactions across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X] Rival IQ's 2025 Social Media Industry Benchmark Report (read via headless browser; its medians are in chart images, so numbers below are text-only): Instagram engagement rate per post (engagement ÷ followers) fell 16% YoY across all industries; "Carousels outperformed Reels in engagement this year"; median Instagram posting frequency decreased slightly "for the first time in three years" while Influencers raised theirs ~20%; Higher Ed led Instagram engagement despite below-median posting ("more isn't always better on Instagram"). Retail and Home Decor engagement down nearly 30%. — [Rival IQ 2025 report](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/)
- [Measured, secondary] Rival IQ's all-industry per-follower median Instagram engagement is reported as 0.30% (2026 edition) by one source and 0.36% (2025 edition) by others; top-quartile ~1.05%. The 2025 report page itself prints the median only in a chart image, and a 2026 edition could not be located on rivaliq.com or quid.com in this pass. — [Apaya](https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-instagram); [inmagic](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/)
- [Gap] Hootsuite and Later publish 2026 benchmark pages but no median reach-rate figure for small accounts surfaced in search; their engagement figures (~3%) use different formulas (per-reach/impressions) and are not comparable to per-follower medians. — [ClickMinded summary](https://www.clickminded.com/social-media-benchmarks/)

**Posting frequency vs reach**
- [Measured, 18 Feb 2026; ~50K posts, Jan 2026] The Shield Index: for 5–10K-follower accounts, 1–3 posts/month gave 15.9% typical reach rate vs 9% at 16+ posts/month ("Higher frequency consistently lowers typical reach rate"), while heavier posters had more "breakout" (top-1%) posts. Breakout percentages (e.g. 810%) are not explained; single-month sample. — [The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/january-2026-post-frequency-vs-reach)

### Inferences
- The datasets agree on direction: for a page under 10K followers, Reels are the reach format and carousels the engagement/saves format; a mix (Reels for discovery, carousels for saves) is what the numbers support.
- Frequency: per-post reach falls as you post more, but total reach and growth rise; Socialinsider's small-account average is 8–10 Reels a month, Metricool's growth curve favours more. For a two-person business, 3–5 Reels/week is the range both datasets would call "above average" without entering diminishing returns.
- The 30–60 s band being the best reach band (Socialinsider H1 2026) matches the 30–70 s educational format in the brief.

### Gaps
- No study reports a median **save rate** or **share rate** (Socialinsider explicitly excludes them as private metrics); only average saves per post exist.
- No benchmark isolates "new" (0–1K follower) business accounts beyond Metricool's 0–1K tier, for which no figures were published in the release.
- Rival IQ's 2025 report was read (headless browser) but its medians are chart images; Hootsuite, Later (later.com/blog/instagram-reels-benchmarks/ rendered as an empty page) and Sprout Social primary reports were not reached; figures come from secondary summaries. PetaPixel returned 403 to WebFetch and was recorded as inaccessible (not retried).
- All engagement benchmarks use likes+comments ÷ followers; none measure Mosseri's "sends per reach", so there is no public benchmark for the signal Instagram says matters most for non-followers.

## KQ3. Hook and retention craft for 30–70 s educational Reels

### Takeaway
The only official numbers are structural: Instagram defines Skip Rate as leaving within 3 seconds and says a "flatter" retention line is the goal; measured data shows ~65% of plays on sub-10K accounts are skipped within 3 s (Socialinsider H1 2026) and 30–60 s Reels get the best reach rate. Everything about text hooks, pattern interrupts, loops and captions-on-screen is practitioner opinion with no published controlled study for Instagram.

### Cited Findings
- [Official, 25 Aug 2025] Skip Rate = share of viewers who leave within the first 3 seconds; Retention chart shows where viewers drop. Instagram: "the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience is." — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992)
- [Measured, H1 2026] Skip rate 65.5% (1–5K followers), 65.3% (5–10K); reach rate peaks at 30–60 s and falls steadily beyond 90 s. — [Socialinsider Reels Statistics](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)
- [Vendor] Metricool: there is "no official Instagram source (so far) on what percentage equals good performance" for skip rate; use it to understand behaviour, not to hit a target. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)
- [Vendor/opinion] One creator argues the hook must land in the first ~1.5 s, before Instagram overlays the caption, and should be tested with sound off. — [Async blog](https://async.com/blog/reel-retention-tips/)
- [Vendor/opinion] Hook-rate tiers (e.g. "<20% watching past 3 s is below average") circulate but are unsourced. — [NestScale](https://nestscale.com/?p=47200)
- [Vendor/opinion] Blogs recommend 15–30 s for educational Reels and 30–60 s for tutorials, with "70%+ retention" figures that come from one blog's own analysis, not a named study; the "69% watch muted" statistic appears without an origin. — [Opus.pro](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length); [Klap](https://klap.app/blog/how-long-should-reels-be); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/instagram-reels-look-like-to-perform-well/)
- [Official, 14 Oct 2025 via Haberman] Mosseri: carousels get re-served to followers who swiped past them, which is the only official "second chance" mechanic mentioned; no equivalent stated for Reels. — [ICYMI](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)
- [Measured, 2025] Metricool/HypeAuditor recommend Trial Reels specifically to "refine hooks and formats" before posting to followers. — [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/)

### Inferences
- With two-thirds of small-account plays skipped inside 3 s, the opening frame (on-screen text + first spoken sentence) is the single largest controllable variable; Instagram's own Retention chart is now the measurement tool, so each Reel's drop-off points can be read directly rather than guessed.
- For a 30–70 s educational Reel, the measured reach data argues for staying ≤60 s unless the extra length is needed; the brief's 45–50 s clarity target sits inside the best band.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed or large-sample study on text hooks, pattern interrupts, loops or burned-in captions on Instagram was found; all guidance is anecdotal/vendor.
- No published "good" skip-rate or average-watch-time benchmark from Instagram.

## KQ4. "Comment X and I'll DM you" lead magnets: native vs ManyChat, policy, results

### Takeaway
Comment-keyword-to-DM automation is permitted when it runs through Meta's official Messaging API on your own posts (ManyChat and similar); Instagram/Meta Business Suite's native tools cover instant replies and away messages, and native keyword/comment-triggered DMs are either absent or limited and inconsistently described. Meta's Spam policy bans **engagement-gating** ("requiring users to engage... to access exclusive content") and very-high-frequency automated engagement, which is the real policy edge for "comment GUIDE" funnels. ManyChat moved to a five-tier, active-contact-based pricing on 2 Mar 2026 with a 25-contact free plan. All reported conversion figures are vendor claims.

### Cited Findings

**Policy**
- [Official, Meta Community Standards, Spam; latest dated revision 26 Jun 2024] Prohibited: "Requiring or claiming that users are required to engage with content (e.g., liking, sharing)" before they can view promised content; "Like/share-gating": requiring users to engage "in the form of likes, shares, follows, or any other public-facing form of engagement" to access exclusive content; engaging with content or creating accounts "either manually or automatically, at very high frequencies" (no numeric threshold; lower-frequency accounts may also be restricted when other spam signals exist); offering cash or anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement. The policy does not use the term "engagement bait" and does not mention comment-to-DM. — [Meta Transparency Center, Spam](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)
- [Official, via press, Apr 2025] Meta said comments detected as coordinated fake engagement "will be seen less" and reduced reach for spammy posts/accounts. — [MediaNama, Apr 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/04/223-facebook-cuts-reach-fake-accounts-spam-content/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-algorithm-changes-combat-spam-posts-comments/746328/)
- [Vendor, 2026] Vendors agree: comment-to-DM on your own posts via the official API is permitted "within limits"; bots that comment on other people's posts, or tools that log into your account/bypass the API, risk restriction or suspension; automated DMs count toward Instagram's sending limits. — [Inro, comment-to-DM](https://inro.social/blog/instagram-comment-to-dm-automation); [Inro, auto replies](https://www.inro.social/blog/how-to-set-instagram-auto-replies); [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-comment-to-dm-automation)
- [Opinion, 2026] Critical view: "comment GUIDE" funnels train audiences to post one-word comments, erode comments as a feedback signal and nudge the account toward engagement bait, though "a real asset behind a real keyword is a fair trade." — [Inro, DM automation guide 2026](https://inro.social/blog/instagram-dm-automation-guide-2026)

**Native vs third-party**
- [Vendor, 2026] Inro's native-vs-tool table: Instagram/Meta Business Suite natively offers one Instant Reply and one Away Message (500-character cap, first-name variable), both off by default; "Reply to a comment on a post" and "different replies for different questions" marked **No** natively. — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/how-to-set-instagram-auto-replies)
- [Vendor, conflicting] Other vendor guides say Business Suite (desktop) offers "custom keyword" automated DM responses for up to five keywords, sent after 15 minutes unless you reply first, and that Meta tested a "Reply to Keywords" option for Instagram **ad** comments (up to five keywords triggering a private message). — [Omnichat](https://blog.omnichat.ai/instagram-chatbot-auto-reply/); [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/meta-tests-auto-dm-replies-for-instagram-ad-comments/). Could not verify either against Meta's help centre.
- [Vendor, Mar 2026] ManyChat moved to five tiers on 2 Mar 2026 and cut its free plan; billing is by "active contacts" per month. Prices reported as $0 / $17 / $39 / $99 / $199 monthly by one source and $0 / from $14 / from $29 / $69 / $139 (annual billing) by another; free = 25 active contacts, 4 automations, branding; Essential 250 contacts (+$0.10 each over); Pro 2,500 contacts (+$0.05) with AI features; Business 7,500 contacts, 5 seats, Instagram+Messenger+WhatsApp together; Advanced 25,000. ### Inferences
- The defensible setup: a genuine asset, keyword request phrased as an offer not a gate ("comment GUIDE if you want the PDF" rather than "comment to unlock"), delivery via a Meta-API tool, and a public reply so the comment thread still contains real conversation. That stays inside the Spam policy's wording.
- For a small Indian business, ManyChat's free tier (25 active contacts/month) will be exhausted by one moderately successful Reel; budget for Essential/Pro or pick an India-based alternative (WATI, bot.space and others sell Instagram-to-WhatsApp handoff).

## KQ5. India specifics: Reels consumption, timing (IST), Hinglish and regional language, small-business growth

### Takeaway
Official and third-party data agree India is Instagram's heaviest Reels market (Meta: India leads Reels watch time and Reels produced; Mosseri: "much higher sharing, more time spent, and greater engagement from Indian users"; Sensor Tower/BofA: ~49 h 42 min per user per month on Instagram).
