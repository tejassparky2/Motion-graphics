## What it means
1. **The growth is Instagram, not YouTube.** The same videos did 100-800 times better on Instagram (2.8M vs 5.9K).
   Only two did well on YouTube (HIV, radiation). Instagram pushes reels that get saved and sent in DMs; health
   "this is inside YOU" topics get forwarded to family.
2. **Winning topic = a hidden thing in your own body, said in Hinglish as a question.** "Why do your palms sweat when
   you're not hot?", "The chickenpox virus is still inside 9 out of 10 of you", "HIV hides for 10 years". Animal,
   business and space topics (jewel wasp 1K, Jio IPO 802) did not take off.
3. **Few posts, each one built like a mini documentary.** 12 posts total, ~2-2.5 min each (the HIV reel is 2:26),
   one narrator, one metaphor carried all the way (body = country, immune system = army).
4. **One look, kept every time:** vintage printed poster on creased paper, muted colour per section, small white
   line + big red word as the headline, flip counters, "FIG." labels. Instagram grid covers are separate,
   photo-real images with a 2-3 word Hinglish question ("SAB THEEK HAI?", "PHIR BHI PASEENA?!").
5. **Titles/covers ask a question the viewer has felt themselves** (sweat, stress, the Sun) and promise a hidden
   cause, with a shock word: "Kaise", "Kyun", "Agar... toh kya hoga?" + 😳.
