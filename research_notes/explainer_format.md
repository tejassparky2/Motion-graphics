# New explainer format (owner request, 24 Oct 2026): reference structure

The owner wants English explainer videos in the style of instagram.com/meanwhilemayank (Hindi). Structure notes on
their reference reel (HIV/AIDS, 2 min 26 s), analysed in the Interestingly Strange chat. **Structure reference only:
never copy its script, drawings or topic treatment.** The reel itself is not in the repo (public repo).

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-13 s | Hook | Mystery: something hides in your body for years, no symptoms, then it's too late |
| 13-22 s | Stakes + name | Big number on a flip counter over a world map, then the name |
| 22-25 s | Turn | "First, let's understand what it actually does." |
| 25-50 s | Metaphor setup | Body = country, immune system = army, CD4 cells = commanders |
| 50-67 s | Mechanism | Virus enters, writes its code into the DNA, the cell becomes a virus factory, dies, chain repeats |
| 67-91 s | Timeline | Mild flu, then years of seeming normal while the war goes on, then the final stage |
| 91-102 s | Reframe | It doesn't kill you directly; other infections do |
| 102-106 s | Origin | Where it came from |
| 106-123 s | Myth-busting | How it spreads, and how it does NOT |
| 123-137 s | Hope | Treatment today and what it makes possible |
| 137-146 s | Close | How many people live with it worldwide and locally |

What makes it work: one mystery hook, stakes with a big number, one metaphor carried all the way, then a timeline, a
reframe, myth-busting, and an ending on hope.

Look: vintage printed poster on creased paper with grain, a muted palette per section, flat clean illustrations,
a two-line headline per beat (small white line + big red condensed word), mechanical flip counters, "health report"
cards with stamps, a new image or headline every 1-2 s.

Numbers must be re-checked against WHO/UNAIDS/CDC/NHS before use (e.g. UNAIDS: about 40 million living with HIV,
about 42 million deaths to date; U=U per CDC/WHO).
