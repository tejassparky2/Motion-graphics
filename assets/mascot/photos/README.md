# The owner's home photos for the Vex series (owner, 3 Nov 2026: "keep photos in project")

Originals were phone shots (4000x3000); here they are auto-rotated, resized to 1500x2000 and stripped of all
metadata (EXIF, camera data) before committing, because the repo is public. `crops/` holds the 720x1280 frames the
videos use (centre crop to 9:16, a little brighter).

- floor_living: living-room tiles, flowers and the hall door at the top.
- floor_swing: the swing-chair corner on artificial grass.
- floor_workshop: the workshop desk with the 3D printers (a "lab" set for later episodes).
- hand_palm: the keeper's open palm over the floor (offers Vex something).
- hand_reach: the keeper's hand reaching in (stops or grabs Vex).
