# Milo thrillers, batch 1: scripts for approval (14 Oct 2026)

Owner: "as scary as possible... suspense thriller creepy, not only horror or ghost... keep the viewer hooked with
both story and visuals... the main character in every video." (Replaces out/scripts_creepy_batch1.md.)

**Series:** every episode stars **Milo** (15, messy black hair, freckles, faded red hoodie), told by him in first
person. Real-world dread, no ghosts: intruders, strangers, secrets, things that could actually happen. Each one is
~45 s, opens on a scene where something is already wrong, keeps a new clue or shock every few seconds, and ends on an
either/or question that makes people comment. Kept to psychological fear (no gore), so the videos stay ad-friendly.

**Look:** film-like realistic shots (made locally, free), dark teal-and-amber grade, crushed blacks, film grain, slow
push-ins and drifts on every shot, light flicker, a cut or punch-in about every 1-1.5 s. Phone texts, notes and
labels are drawn crisp by our engine on top (image models can't write text). Sound: low drone, heartbeat that speeds
up, sudden silence before each shock. Voice: Turbo (narrator Milo + character voices), tags only on the shocks.

## 1. "Mom Never Calls Me Sweetie" (~44 s)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Come down for dinner, sweetie." But my mom has never called me sweetie. | Frame 0: Milo frozen at the top of a dark staircase, warm kitchen light below. Hook text: **MOM NEVER CALLS ME SWEETIE** |
| 2 | The kitchen light is on. And someone down there is humming her favorite song. | Slow push down the stairs toward the glowing doorway; a shadow moving on the kitchen wall |
| 3 | Then my phone buzzes. A text from Mom. | Milo's face lit blue by the phone |
| 4 | "Working late. Order pizza. Love you." | Phone screen overlay, the text types in |
| 5 | The humming stops. | Hard cut to the empty kitchen doorway; all sound drops out |
| 6 | "Milo? I can hear you breathing." | A woman's silhouette steps into the light at the bottom of the stairs; face in shadow |
| 7 | One step. Then another. She's coming up. | Low angle: a foot on the first stair; heartbeat starts |
| 8 | I lock myself in my room. And I call Mom. | Door slams, lock turns; Milo dialling, hands shaking |
| 9 | Her ringtone starts playing. Right under my bed. | Slow push toward the dark gap under the bed; a phone screen glowing in there |
| 10 | Someone in my room has her phone. Someone in the hall is wearing her voice. | Split: under-bed glow / a shadow under the bedroom door |
| 11 | The doorknob turns. And the bed creaks. | Doorknob turning; the bed frame shifts |
| 12 | Door, or window? You have three seconds. | Two cards: DOOR / WINDOW, a 3-2-1 countdown |

Voices: Milo (Turbo stock); the woman downstairs (Turbo, warm female reference, a touch too calm). Tags: [gasp] on 9.
**Titles:** "Mom Never Calls Me Sweetie..." · "Her Phone Was Ringing Under My Bed" · "Door or Window? 3 Seconds."

## 2. "He Pointed Behind Me" (~46 s) (based on real "phrogging" cases: strangers secretly living in people's homes)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every night at [three a.m.|3 A.M.], the old man across the street stands at his window. Watching my house. | Frame 0: from Milo's dark bedroom window, the neighbour's lit window across the street, an old man standing still. Hook text: **HE WATCHES MY HOUSE EVERY NIGHT** |
| 2 | Last night, I waved at him. He didn't wave back. | Milo's hand at the glass; the old man doesn't move |
| 3 | He pointed. Not at me. Behind me. | Close on the old man's arm slowly rising, finger pointing |
| 4 | I turned around. My closet door was open. Just a crack. | Slow turn to the closet; a black gap in the door |
| 5 | When I looked back, he was pressing a note to his glass. | The old man flattening paper against his window |
| 6 | Three words. "Don't. Turn. Around." | Note overlay, one word per beat |
| 7 | [gasp] The floor creaked behind me. | Extreme close on Milo's eyes; a floorboard behind him |
| 8 | I ran. I didn't stop until I was banging on his door. | Fast cuts: stairs, front door, the street, the neighbour's door |
| 9 | The police searched our house all night. | Red and blue lights sweeping the house front |
| 10 | In the attic above my closet, they found a sleeping bag. Empty cans. And forty photos. | Flashlight beam in the attic: sleeping bag, cans, a wall of photos |
| 11 | Of me. Sleeping. | Photos close-up: Milo asleep, from above (drawn so no real person) |
| 12 | Someone had been living in our house for weeks. | Attic hatch above the closet, slowly swinging |
| 13 | And the old man had seen him every night. So why did he wait until last night to warn me? | The old man at his window again, not moving; question card |

Voices: Milo (Turbo stock). The old man never speaks (scarier). Tags: [gasp] on 7.
**Titles:** "He Pointed Behind Me..." · "Someone Lived in Our Attic for Weeks" · "The Note Said: Don't. Turn. Around."

## 3. "Never Open the Basement Door" (~45 s)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My first babysitting job came with one rule. Never open the basement door. | Frame 0: a basement door with three locks, a note taped to it. Hook text: **RULE 1: NEVER OPEN THE BASEMENT** |
| 2 | At ten p.m., something knocked. From inside the basement. | The door in the dark; the knocks drawn as rings; the locks rattle |
| 3 | Lily, the little girl, didn't even look up from her drawing. "That's just my brother." | Lily (6) at the kitchen table, crayons, calm |
| 4 | But her parents told me Lily is an only child. | Family photo on the fridge: mom, dad, Lily. Only three |
| 5 | The knocking stopped. Then a whisper through the door. "Are they gone?" | Milo's ear near the door; light under it flickers |
| 6 | A boy's voice. "Please. They said you'd be nice." | A small shadow moving in the gap under the door |
| 7 | My phone lit up. A text from Lily's mom. | Phone glow on Milo's face |
| 8 | "Everything okay? Remember the rule." | Text overlay types in |
| 9 | How did she know? | Milo slowly looking up |
| 10 | Then I saw it. A tiny red light in the corner of the ceiling. A camera. Pointed right at me. | Slow push to the ceiling corner; blinking red light |
| 11 | [chuckle] Lily smiled. "Mommy's always watching." | Lily looking up from her drawing, smiling; her drawing: four stick figures, one behind a door |
| 12 | So. Do you open the door? Or pretend you never heard it? | Two cards: OPEN IT / PRETEND |

Voices: Milo (Turbo stock); Lily and the boy (Turbo, young references); tags: [chuckle] (Lily) on 11.
**Titles:** "Never Open the Basement Door" · "Her Parents Said She Was an Only Child" · "The Babysitting Rule I Broke"
