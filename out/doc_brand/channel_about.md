## Channel settings
- **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids". The videos show surgery and blood, and are aimed at teens and adults.
- **Category:** Education (or Science & Technology).
- **Country:** set yours. It affects which ads and which recommendations you get.
- **Default upload settings:** paste the disclaimer line ("Cartoon, real medicine. Not medical advice: talk to a doctor about your own health.") into the default description.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** the organ voices are AI text-to-speech and the doctor is your own cloned voice. YouTube asks you to tick "altered or synthetic content" when realistic AI voices could mislead viewers. These are cartoons, so it's usually not needed, but if in doubt, tick it; it doesn't hurt reach.
