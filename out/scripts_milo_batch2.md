# Milo series, batch 2: scripts for approval (supernatural + horror mix)

Owner, 14 Oct 2026: "not abandon ghost... use all ways", as scary as possible, visuals as strong as the story.
Batch 1 (intruder, attic, basement) was real-world thriller, so batch 2 leans supernatural. Same engine as batch 1:
something wrong in sentence 1, a new shock every few seconds, silence before each reveal, an ending the viewer has to
decide. ~45 s each, Milo narrates, no gore.

---

## 4. "My Twin Came Home Wrong" (supernatural, ~46 s)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My twin brother Leo came home from camp. And he hugged Mom with the wrong arm. | Frame 0: the front door at dusk, two boys with duffel bags, Mom hugging one. Hook: **HE CAME BACK WRONG** |
| 2 | Leo is left-handed. This Leo does everything with his right. | Quick cuts: right hand on a fork, a toothbrush, a door handle |
| 3 | His freckles are on the wrong cheek. His hair parts on the wrong side. | Old photo of Leo vs Leo now, side by side, a mirror-flip wipe |
| 4 | Our dog won't go near him. She just growls from under the table. | Dog's glowing eyes under the table, teeth showing |
| 5 | That night, I hold up our old photo to the bathroom mirror. | Milo in a dark bathroom, the photo held up to the mirror |
| 6 | In the mirror, the Leo in the photo looks exactly like the Leo downstairs. | Mirror close-up: the photo's reflection matches the new Leo |
| 7 | Then the reflection of my own hand moves. Before I do. | Milo frozen; in the mirror, his hand lifts first. Sting |
| 8 | Behind me, Leo leans on the door. "Took you long enough." | Leo in the doorway, half his face in shadow, smiling |
| 9 | "Everyone comes back through the mirror eventually." | Close on Leo's eyes |
| 10 | I look down. I'm holding the photo in my left hand. | Milo's left hand, the photo shaking |
| 11 | I have never been left-handed. | Mirror: Milo's reflection smiling while he isn't |
| 12 | So which side of the mirror am I on now? | Split frame: Milo / his reflection, a "?" |

**Titles:** "My Twin Came Home Wrong..." · "His Freckles Were on the Wrong Cheek" · "Which Side of the Mirror Am I On?"

## 5. "Room 4 Is Always Empty" (ghost / rules horror, ~45 s)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My uncle's motel has one rule for the night desk. If room 4 calls, don't answer. | Frame 0: Milo at a motel desk at night, a phone with a red light on "4". Hook: **IF ROOM 4 CALLS, DON'T ANSWER** |
| 2 | Room 4 has been empty for twenty years. | Dusty door "4", "OUT OF ORDER" sign, cobwebs |
| 3 | At [two a.m.|2 A.M.], the desk phone rings. Room 4. | Clock flips to 2:00; the "4" light blinks red; ring |
| 4 | I let it ring. Eleven times. Then it stops. | Phone shaking with each ring, then silence |
| 5 | Then my cell phone rings. It's my uncle. | Milo's phone: "Uncle Ray" |
| 6 | "Milo, I'm in room 4. The door locked behind me. Come let me out." | Uncle's voice, too calm; static under it |
| 7 | I look out the office window. My uncle's truck isn't in the lot. | Empty parking lot in the rain, one flickering lamp |
| 8 | And in the window of room 4, the curtain moves. | The curtain of room 4 lifting slightly; a pale hand |
| 9 | My cell phone buzzes again. A text. From my uncle. | Phone glow on Milo's face |
| 10 | "Don't answer room 4. I'm at home. Lock the office." | Text overlay |
| 11 | Behind me, the office door clicks. Unlocked. | The lock turning by itself |
| 12 | Two uncles. One is home. One is in room 4. Which one is really him? | Two cards: HOME / ROOM 4 |

**Titles:** "If Room 4 Calls, Don't Answer" · "Room 4 Has Been Empty for 20 Years" · "My Uncle Called From Room 4"

## 6. "The Last Trick-or-Treater" (Halloween ghost story for Oct 31, ~45 s)

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At midnight on Halloween, one last trick-or-treater knocked. A little girl in a white sheet. | Frame 0: porch light, a small sheet ghost at the door, empty street. Hook: **SHE HAD NO SHOES** |
| 2 | Her sheet had real eye holes. And she had no shoes. | Close: bare grey feet on the welcome mat, frost around them |
| 3 | "Trick or treat," she whispered. "Can I come in? It's cold." | Sheet ghost tilting its head |
| 4 | Every kid on our street knows the rule. You never invite them in. | Milo's hand on the door frame, the bowl of candy |
| 5 | I held out the candy bowl instead. She didn't take any. | Bowl held out; the small hand doesn't move |
| 6 | "That's okay," she said. "Your sister already let me in." | Sting; Milo turns |
| 7 | I don't have a sister. | Hallway behind Milo, empty |
| 8 | Then I heard giggling. From upstairs. | Stairs up into darkness; small wet footprints going up |
| 9 | I looked back at the door. The sheet was lying empty on the porch. | The sheet flat on the mat, no one under it |
| 10 | And the footprints on the stairs led to my room. | Footprints ending at Milo's bedroom door, light under it |
| 11 | Go upstairs, or run out the front door? | Two cards: UPSTAIRS / RUN |

**Titles:** "The Last Trick-or-Treater Had No Shoes" · "Never Invite Them In on Halloween" · "She Said My Sister Let Her In"
