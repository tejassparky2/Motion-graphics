# Gemini voice prompts (your own voice clone)

One prompt per video, in upload order. Each script is word-for-word what the animation is built around, so the
visuals land on the right words.

## How to use

1. Paste one prompt into Gemini with your avatar. Generate the video.
2. **Listen once.** If it skipped, added or changed any words, generate it again. Small pauses are fine: the
   animation stretches to follow your timing.
3. Download it and **rename the file to the video name** shown in each heading, e.g. `zombie_ant.mp4`.
   Audio files work too (`.m4a`, `.wav`, `.mp3`).
4. Upload it here. I sync the animation to your voice, re-render, check it, and send it back.

**If Gemini can't do the whole script in one clip:** stop at the `PART 2` line and make a second clip from
there. Name them `zombie_ant_1.mp4`, `zombie_ant_2.mp4`, and I join them in order.

**Only the voice is used.** The avatar's picture isn't shown, and the cartoon stays as it is.

**Watermark:** Gemini output carries Google's SynthID watermark. I leave it untouched (I never remove
provenance watermarks). It is your own voice narrating your own script, which is normal, allowed use.

**Timing:** anything due within the next few hours is safer uploaded in the current narrator voice.
Do the later slots first.
