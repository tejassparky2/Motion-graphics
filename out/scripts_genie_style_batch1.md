# Genie-style stories, batch 1 (for approval)

Same formula as the genie video: a character with ONE rule, a kid who turns it against itself with one clever line,
both outcomes in two short sentences, a funny callback, and an either/or question. All original stories (no facts to
check), about 40 s each, with character voices. Each opens mid-scene with the hook in big text (our hook rules).

## 1. The Robot That Must Obey (~40 s)
Title: **1 Order Broke This Robot 🤖** · On screen: a shiny home robot in a messy bedroom; big text "IT MUST OBEY"
| # | Who | Line | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Narrator | A robot has one rule. It must obey every order. | Robot salutes; rule card "OBEY EVERY ORDER" |
| 2 | Robot | Give me an order. I will obey. | Robot's eyes glow |
| 3 | Narrator | Clean the room. Done. | Mess whooshes away; tick |
| 4 | Narrator | Do the homework. Done. | Pages flip; tick |
| 5 | Narrator | Then the kid smiles, and gives one more order. | Kid grins |
| 6 | Kid | Disobey this order. | Big text "DISOBEY THIS ORDER" |
| 7 | Narrator | The robot freezes. | Robot locks up; ice |
| 8 | Narrator | If it obeys, it has to disobey. So it breaks the rule. | Card "OBEY → DISOBEY ✗" |
| 9 | Narrator | If it disobeys, that's exactly what the order said. So it obeyed. | Card "DISOBEY → OBEYED ✗" |
| 10 | Narrator | Either way, it can't follow its own rule. | The two cards knot together |
| 11 | Narrator | Smoke comes out of its ears. Beep. | Smoke puffs; "BEEP" |
| 12 | Narrator | Meanwhile, the homework is done. Perfectly. | Kid relaxing, A+ paper |
| 13 | Narrator | So what should the robot do? Obey, or disobey? | Buttons: OBEY / DISOBEY |

## 2. The Last Bencher's One Sentence (~42 s)

|---|---|---|---|
