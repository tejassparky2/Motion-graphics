**Facts and sources:**
- Up to 400 mg of caffeine a day is generally not linked to harmful effects in healthy adults; toxic effects such as
  seizures can appear with rapid intake of around 1,200 mg (US FDA, "Spilling the Beans: How Much Caffeine Is Too
  Much?").
- A large (16 oz) Monster Energy has about 160 mg of caffeine (manufacturer label).
- Caffeine works by blocking adenosine receptors; its half-life in adults is about 5 hours (typical range 3-7).
- The American Academy of Pediatrics says children and teens shouldn't drink energy drinks.
- High doses of caffeine can trigger heart-rhythm problems (arrhythmias); deaths are rare and usually involve
  large, fast doses or heart conditions, so the video shows a cartoon collapse, not a claim that 8 cans kill.
