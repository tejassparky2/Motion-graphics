## 2. Formats: what works and what doesn't
| Format | Stayed % (average) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher vs student riddles / school twists (Last Row Kid, Teacher Bluffed, Excuses) | ~72 | **Best.** Make more Last Bencher. |
| One-sentence "impossible" ideas (Infinite Hotel, Pinocchio) | ~73 | **Best.** Simple setup, instant "wait, what?" |
| Clever trickster history (Double Agent, Archimedes, Shortest War) | ~67 | Good |
| Math / probability paradoxes (Monty Hall, Birthday, Potato, Horn, Bootstrap, Ship) | ~50 | **Weak.** People swipe before the logic pays off |
| Weird animals (Zombie Ant, Emus, Beetle) | ~45 | Weak so far |
| "One word" history comebacks (Sparta, Pyrrhic) | ~54 | Middling |

## 4. What this means for the queue (#26–46)
- **Strong fits for the winning formats:** Last Bencher 4 (#36), the crocodile (#41), the court (#40), the barber (#42), the genie (#46), the spear (#43),
  and Mark Twain (#38): all teacher/student or one-sentence logic traps or clever tricksters.
- **Riskier:** math-heavy ones (Achilles #37, potato-style) and animals (honeybee #35, jellyfish #39). They already open on the
  craziest claim, but expect lower numbers.
- **Comments come from debates:** Hotel (40 comments), Birthday (17) and Pinocchio (11) all end on a question people argue about.
  The new videos all do this.

## 5. Next steps
1. **More of what works:** a new Last Bencher episode every 3–4 days, plus one-sentence logic traps like Pinocchio and the genie.
2. **Fewer math explainers;** when we do one, show the answer's "impossible" result in the first second.
3. **Per-second curves for 2 Shorts** (one at a time, as explained before): *This Hotel Is FULL* (best) and *The Mystery Kid in the Class
   Group Chat* (worst with real views). They'll show the exact second people swipe, so the openings can be fixed.
