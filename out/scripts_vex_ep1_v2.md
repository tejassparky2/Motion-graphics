**How it works now:**
- **Vex** speaks cute gibberish (about 400 Hz, like Grim). What he means shows as red handwriting over his head.
- **The keeper (you, off screen)** answers him and corrects him. Every fact comes out as an answer to something Vex
  asks or does wrong.
- **Look:** one close, bright shot of your living-room floor; no music, no graphics. Plain comic captions show only the
  keeper's lines.
- **Vex** is the code-drawn one, so he can act every line.

**Title:** "Never Let a Vampire Bite a Pufferfish 🐡"
Alt titles: "My Vampire Found a Snack. It Was a Pufferfish. 🐡" / "Vex vs the Pufferfish 🐡"
**Length:** about 40-45 s. **Outcome:** the animal wins (Vex loses twice, then gives up).

**Facts (sources as before):**
- Cooking doesn't destroy the toxin, and there's no antidote: UMEM Educational Pearls #2689; Hong Kong Centre for
  Food Safety, Food Safety Focus no. 178 (2021).
- Bacteria make it, and it builds up through the food chain: Noguchi & Arakawa, Marine Drugs (2008).
- Farmed fish carry far less, but it's not zero: CFS Hong Kong (2021); Singapore Food Agency.
- In Japan only licensed chefs may serve it.
