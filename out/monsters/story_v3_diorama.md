## Animation-quality pass (2.5D, all shots)
- **Clearer acting:** every character move gets a clear key pose, anticipation before it and overshoot after it, on
  2s, with no stiff poses. Kai's face is closer on the key beats (15, 17, 22, 26-30).
- **Kai reads as lying down:** a bent knee, an arm flung over his head and grass round him in every chest shot (some
  top-down frames read as standing).
- **The shrimp acts:** a narrowed glare when it aims (15), a startled hop when the claw goes "pff" (22), and a smug
  strut back to the puddle (23).
- **The bird acts:** a head tilt and a lock-on to the shrimp (26), then a dive whose shadow grows over it (27-29).
- **Camera:** a slow push-in on every shot; the snap fires straight along the line from the shrimp to Kai.
- **Phone-size readability:** the subject of each line is the biggest thing in frame and clear of the caption band.

## Sources (the real facts, unchanged)
- Pistol shrimp: the collapsing bubble briefly reaches about 4,700 °C (Lohse et al., Nature 2001; Versluis et al.,
  Science 2000), against about 5,500 °C at the sun's surface, and it only forms in water.
- Shrikes ("butcher birds") impale prey on thorns to store it.
