# New series, for your approval: world + Episode 1 script

Nothing is rendered until you approve or change this. Change any line you like: your words stay.

## The world in 6 lines

- **Series name (working):** *Cute Things Kill Here*
- **The world:** a pastel, cozy-looking land of soft hills, candy-coloured mushrooms and quiet ponds. Every creature
  in it is small and adorable, and every one has a power that can flatten a forest. The locals live behind bells and
  walls, and they never run from small things.
- **The hero:** **Kai**, a wildlife-biology student from Earth. He died yesterday and woke up here. He has no powers. His
  weapon is real animal science: each creature's terrifying power works like a real animal's surprising ability, so
  Kai knows its limit if he can work out which animal it's like.
- **How taming works (our own rule, nothing like a capture ball):** a creature bonds with the first person who
  *survives* its power. A glowing ring appears on the wrist. So every new partner means surviving something terrifying
  by thinking, not by luck.
- **Every creature has three things:**
  - a cute body;
  - one creepy *tell* you can spot before the power fires;
  - a hard *limit* and a *cost* that Kai can use.
- **Episodes:** about 70 s Shorts with a game look: HP bar, "WILD ???" tag, a two-choice menu, "BOND!" pop-ups. Every
  episode ends on an either/or question. Episodes rotate outcomes (win, costly win, creature refuses, rival wins), so
  no two in a row end the same way.
