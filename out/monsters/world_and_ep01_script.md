# New series, for your approval: world + Episodes 1 and 2

Nothing is rendered until you approve or change this. Change any line you like: your words stay.

## The world in 6 lines

- **Series name (working):** *Cute Things Kill Here*
- **The world:** a pastel, cozy-looking land of soft hills, candy-coloured mushrooms and quiet ponds. Every creature
  in it is small and adorable, and every one has a power that can flatten a forest. The locals live behind bells and
  walls, and they never run from small things.
- **The hero:** **Kai**, a wildlife-biology student from Earth. He dies on Earth and is reborn in this world. He has no
  powers. His weapon is real animal science: each creature's terrifying power works like a real animal's surprising
  ability, so Kai can find its limit once he works out which animal it's like.
- **How taming works (our own rule, nothing like a capture ball):** a creature bonds with the first person who
  *survives* its power, and a glowing ring appears on the wrist. Every new partner means surviving something
  terrifying by thinking, not luck.
- **Every creature has three things:**
  - a cute body;
  - one creepy *tell* you can spot before its power fires;
  - a hard *limit* and a *cost* that Kai can use.
- **Episodes:** about 70-80 s Shorts with a game look: a status bar, "WILD ???" tags, a two-choice menu, "BOND!"
  pop-ups. Every episode ends on an either/or question. Outcomes rotate (win, costly win, creature refuses, rival
  wins), so no two episodes in a row end the same way.

Look: a bright toy-like 3D world with Pokémon-style toon shading and outlines (see the style frame), with the game HUD
on top. A new picture roughly every 2-4 s.

## Notes
- **The creature** is our own design, the aqua "puff-shrimp" with one coral claw. It is not a pink puffball, which would
  look like Kirby or Jigglypuff.
- **The death** happens off-screen: a spark, then black and white. Nothing graphic, so it stays advertiser-friendly.
- **The real fact** (Episode 1 lines 5-6, Episode 2 line 8): a pistol shrimp's snap makes a cavitation bubble that
  briefly reaches about 4,700 °C (about 5,000 K; the sun's surface is about 5,500 °C) and only forms in water (Lohse et
  al., *Nature* 2001; Versluis et al., *Science* 2000).
- **Legal distance:** our own creatures, names, bond rule and HUD wording. No ball, no "-mon" names, and no type icons
  copied from any game.
