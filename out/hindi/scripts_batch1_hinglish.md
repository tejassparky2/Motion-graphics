# Hindi channel: Hinglish scripts, batch 1 (for approval)

The same five videos and animation as the English channel. Only the voice and captions change; the English signs and
cards on screen stay for now. Names are made Indian where they're spoken (and on the name tags). Every sentence is short
and ends with a full stop, like the English rules. Edit any line you want.
