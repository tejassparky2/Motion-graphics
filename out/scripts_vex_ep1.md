**Sources:**
- No antidote; treatment is supportive; cooking, freezing or drying doesn't destroy the toxin: University of Maryland
  emergency medicine notes (UMEM Educational Pearls #2689); Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety, Food Safety Focus
  no. 178 (May 2021).
- The toxin comes mainly from bacteria and builds up through the food chain: Noguchi & Arakawa, "Tetrodotoxin:
  distribution and accumulation in aquatic organisms", Marine Drugs (2008), PMC2525488.
- Farmed fish on clean food carry much less, but are not guaranteed toxin-free (toxin was still found in liver and
  ovary): Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety (2021); Singapore Food Agency, "Pufferfish 101".
- Left out on purpose: "one fish can kill 30 people". Estimates of the lethal dose vary several-fold between
  studies, so the video gives no number.
