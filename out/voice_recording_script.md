# Record your voice (about 2 minutes)

This one recording teaches the model your voice. After that, every future video can be narrated in it.

## How to record (this matters more than the mic)
1. **Quiet room.** Turn off fans and AC, close the windows. A room with a bed, curtains or clothes is better than an empty
   echoey room. Sitting inside a closet full of clothes works surprisingly well.
2. **Phone about one hand-width from your mouth**, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't hit the mic.
3. Use the phone's **voice recorder app** (not WhatsApp, which compresses the audio). Save it as .m4a or .wav.
4. **Speak like you're telling a friend a crazy story:** energetic, clear, not rushed. This is the exact tone the clone
   will copy, so read it the way you want the videos to sound.
5. Leave a **2-second silence** at the start, and don't worry about small mistakes. Just pause and repeat the sentence.
6. Send me the file here.

## Read this

> Hey, welcome to Interestingly Strange. Today's story is one of the weirdest things I've ever read.
>
> In nineteen twenty-five, a con man walked into a fancy hotel in Paris, and sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice.
>
> Wait, what? How is that even possible?
>
> Here's the crazy part. Nobody could believe it. The buyer paid seventy thousand francs, and then he was too
> embarrassed to tell the police!
>
> Now think about this. There are three doors. Behind one, a brand-new car. Behind the other two, goats.
> You pick door number one. Would you switch? Most people say no. They're wrong.
>
> The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs. She sells seashells by the
> seashore, and the shells she sells are surely seashells.
>
> One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. Twenty. Fifty. A hundred. A thousand. A million dollars!
>
> So, what do you think? Genius, or total cheapskate? Let me know in the comments, and I'll see you in the next one.

## What happens next
- I'll make a short **test clip** in your cloned voice for you to approve before we use it anywhere.
- Once you approve, new videos use your voice. The old ones can be re-rendered in it too, if you want.
- **Only your own voice.** Never send someone else's recording to clone. That breaks YouTube's rules, and it isn't
  fair to that person.
