**YouTube description**
```
Naegleria fowleri, the brain-eating amoeba, lives in warm fresh water. It goes up the nose, follows the smell nerves into the brain, and destroys brain tissue. In 2025, Kerala reported about 170 amoeba brain infections and 42 deaths. It's very rare, and early testing and fast treatment saved some patients.

To stay safe:
✅ Hold your nose or wear a nose clip when you jump into fresh water
✅ Don't stir up mud in shallow, warm water
✅ Rinse your nose only with boiled and cooled water
✅ Bad headache and fever after swimming? See a doctor right away, and tell them you swam
❌ It doesn't spread from person to person, and drinking the water doesn't infect you

General information, not medical advice.
```
