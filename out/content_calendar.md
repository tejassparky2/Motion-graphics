## Production notes

- **Length:** 35–50 s. **Narration:** channel voice `am_fenrir`, about 190–210 wpm, no dead air.
- **Every video:** a question or shock hook in the first second, numbers written on screen as they're spoken, a loop
  ending, and an upload sheet (title, alternative titles, description, hashtags, tags, pinned comment).
- **Variety matters for monetization.** YouTube does not pay for mass-produced, repetitive content. Every episode gets its
  own sets, characters and structure, and never reuses another creator's script.
