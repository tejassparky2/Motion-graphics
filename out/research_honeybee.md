# What if you were a honeybee? (fact check, 6 Oct 2026)

Every number the narration uses, with the source checked:

| Claim in the script | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Worker bees are female; summer workers live about 5–7 weeks ("about six weeks") | confirmed | [Worker bee, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker_bee); [American Bee Journal: tasks of a worker](https://americanbeejournal.com/the-tasks-of-a-worker-honey-bee/) |
| A strong colony has up to 60,000 bees | confirmed | [Kansas DOT pollinator facts](https://pollinatorpartners.ksdot.gov/busyAsABee.html); [Beverly Bees](https://www.beverlybees.com/honey-bee-facts/) |
| The queen lays up to 2,000 eggs a day | confirmed | same as above |
| Jobs by age: cleaning cells (incl. her own) → nurse feeding larvae → wax making from glands under the abdomen, comb building → guard checking colony scent → forager at about 3 weeks | confirmed | [Worker bee, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worker_bee); [Buzz About Bees: worker jobs](https://www.buzzaboutbees.net/the-different-jobs-of-worker-honey-bees.html); [Glencomb: six weeks to wing failure](https://glencomb.uk/honey-guide/worker-bee-life-cycle) |
| Wings beat over 200 times a second (sources give 200–230) | confirmed, said as "over two hundred" | [Kansas DOT](https://pollinatorpartners.ksdot.gov/busyAsABee.html) |
| A forager visits 50–100 flowers per trip ("up to a hundred") | confirmed | [Hardy Honey Bee Farms](https://hardyhoney.com/2023/06/20/honey-bee-facts/); [Local Hive](https://localhivehoney.com/blogs/blog/how-much-honey-does-a-bee-make) |
| The waggle dance shows the food's direction as an angle from the sun (Karl von Frisch, Nobel 1973) | confirmed | [Waggle dance, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waggle_dance) |
| Drones have no stinger, don't forage or clean; their one job is mating with a queen; they die right after mating; workers push the rest out in autumn | confirmed | [Drone (bee), Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drone_(bee)); [Blythewood Bee Co.](https://blythewoodbeecompany.com/blogs/news/the-life-of-a-drone-bee) |
| Foragers work until their wings wear out and die in the field | confirmed | [Glencomb](https://glencomb.uk/honey-guide/worker-bee-life-cycle) |
| A worker makes about 1/12 teaspoon of honey in her life (so ~12 bees per teaspoon) | confirmed (an average, per the National Honey Board) | [National Honey Board: honey trivia](https://honey.com/newsroom/presskit/honey-trivia) |
