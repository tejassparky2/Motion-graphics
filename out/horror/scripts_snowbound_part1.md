# Snowbound, Part 1: script v2 for approval (no render until the owner approves)

Rewritten against reports/Horror Shorts hooks retention comments.md (26 Oct 2026). What changed from v1 and why:
- Line 1 is now one sentence, place + impossible detail, 10 words (H4); frame 0 shows the wrong thing with it as
  big text (H2).
- A clock the viewer can see: minus twenty outside, no coat, "twenty minutes" on the HUD counting down (H7, Hitchcock's
  bomb). v1 had no clock.
- Each middle beat removes one explanation: went outside (no footprints, coat), is in another room (Leo "had her hand"),
  Leo is lying (Ivy's video), Ivy is honest (the skipped second) (M2, M3).
- Mid-point re-hook is new information read on screen, not a cut: Ivy's video with its timestamp, then Maya's text (M5).
- The plot loop closes before the vote: Maya's text says she never left the cabin, which answers the no-footprints
  question. Only the judgement stays open: who do you trust (V1, Brewer & Lichtenstein).
- Each side gets one reason tied to a stated fact, equal length (V2, V3); the fork is the literal last line, two
  names (V6). The stake is honesty/trust (V5).
- Three visible clues plus one red herring, each big enough for a phone, none audio-only (M8).

About 70 s, ~195 words. Narrator: the Turbo voice. Leo and Ivy have their own voices. "You" = senses and actions only.
