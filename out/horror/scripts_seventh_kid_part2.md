# The Seventh Kid, Part 2: two versions, for approval (post 29 Oct, 7:30 PM)

Owner, 27 Oct 2026: Halloween first this week, one part a day at 7:30 PM, finale on Saturday 31 Oct. Both versions are
written in advance; once the LEFT/RIGHT comments are in, the one that matches the vote gets rendered.

**The truth (the same in both, fair to every Part 1 clue):**
- **The RIGHT ghost is Sam.** He was holding his breath the whole time ("ghosts don't breathe"), so his breath never
  fogged. "You're silly" was him dodging a name he can't say.
- **The LEFT ghost is the thing.** That's why the eyes caught the light like a cat's, and why it said "Riley"
  perfectly.
- **The note was its trick.** Rule 2 ("it can't say your name") was a lie. Rules 1 and 3 are true.

Both versions:
- open on the vote result;
- pay off the clues the comments argued about;
- add one new rule or fact;
- end on a new kind of question (not "which one is Sam?" again).

About 55-60 s each.

**Checked against the 28 Oct research checklist** (reports/Psychological death game design.md). Version L had a
timing hole. The last porch light went out at line 2, while the thing was holding nobody's hand. Then it was in the
van holding the witch's hand, so rule 3 ("when the porch lights go out, it goes home with the hand it's holding")
didn't add up. It also "watched you go" on the path while it was already in the van. Fixed: one light is still on at
line 2, the path is empty when you look back, and the last light goes out at line 14, while it holds the witch's hand.
