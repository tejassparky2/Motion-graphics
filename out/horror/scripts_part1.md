# Interactive 3D horror: first three episodes (scripts)

Original stories, second person, about 75-85 s each. Narrator: Chatterbox Turbo's stock voice (synthetic; nobody is
cloned). Characters have their own synthetic Turbo voices. Every line is checked with Whisper medium after rendering.
Each episode ends on a two-way choice; the most-commented option decides the next part.

## A. "Floor 13", Part 1
**Title:** Your Elevator Stopped at Floor 13. Your Building Doesn't Have One. 🛗
**HUD:** FLOOR 13 · PART 1, EMERGENCY LIGHT battery 12% → 2%, chips (FLOOR 13 / SIGNAL / DROP -0.5M), rule strip.
**Hidden detail:** Mrs. Gray's feet face backwards, and the mirror never shows her.

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The elevator just stopped at floor 13. Your building doesn't have a floor 13. | Close on your worried face; "13" glows red over the doors; hook text FLOOR 13? |
| 2 | The doors slide open. A dark hallway. Wet footprints, right up to the doors. | Over your shoulder: doors open on a black corridor, wet prints shining |
| 3 | Above the buttons, there's a sign you've never seen before. | The button panel: 12, then 14. No 13 |
| 4 | If the doors open on a floor that isn't there, don't step out. And don't answer. | The sign up close; rule strip appears |
| 5 | The lights die. The emergency light kicks in. Battery, twelve percent. | Lights flicker and die; red emergency lamp; battery meter |
| 6 | Then, a voice from the dark. | The hallway: two eyes shine far down it |
| 7 | (Mrs. Gray) Sweetheart? It's me. Mrs. Gray, from your floor. | Her silhouette, eyes shining |
| 8 | (Mrs. Gray) The elevator is broken. Come out, quickly. I'll take you to the stairs. | She comes closer, leaning on her cane |
| 9 | But Mrs. Gray walks with a cane. She can't do stairs. | Your face, terrified |
| 10-11 | Your phone buzzes. The building group chat. / Elevator broken since six. Nobody use it. | Full-screen chat: "Elevator broken since 6 PM. Nobody use it." |
| 12 | So who pressed the button for 13? | WHO PRESSED 13? |
| 13-14 | A second message. From Mrs. Gray. / Are you in the elevator? Don't come out. I'm at home, on fourteen. | Her chat: typing… then the messages |
| 15 | The voice in the hallway stops. | The hallway is suddenly empty |
| 16 | In the mirror, the hallway behind you is empty. | The mirror: your face, an empty doorway |
| 17 | You turn around. It isn't. | She stands in the doorway, smiling, eyes shining (flash, sting) |
| 18 | The elevator jolts. And drops half a metre. | Shake; the hallway floor jumps up; -0.5 M |
| 19 | (Mrs. Gray, whispering) Come out now. Or you'll fall. | Low angle on her, reaching in |
| 20 | Battery, two percent. | Your face in the failing red light |
| 21 | Step out, and you're alone in the dark with whatever she is. | Her in the doorway |
| 22 | Stay, and you're trusting a cable that just slipped. | The car roof and its cables, from the shaft |
| 23 | Whatever most of you choose happens in Part 2. | |
| 24 | So, do you step out onto floor 13? Or stay in an elevator that's falling? | YOUR CALL. STEP OUT / STAY |

## B. "Last Stop", Night 1

**HUD:** LAST STOP · NIGHT 1, STOPS LEFT meter, PASSENGERS chip 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7, ROUTE 9 chip, clock.
**Hidden detail:** one of the faces reflected behind you is the old man, who is sitting in front of you.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | The driver told you one rule. Whatever you hear, don't look at the back seats. | Your face; hook ONE RULE: DON'T LOOK AT THE BACK SEATS |

| 4 | At the next stop, the doors open. Nobody gets on. | The doors open on an empty, flickering shelter |
| 5 | But the bus dips. Like someone heavy just stepped in. | The bus sinks; PASSENGERS 4 |
| 6 | Behind you, a seat creaks. Then another. | Your face; PASSENGERS 5, 6 |

| 8 | In the dark window, you see your own reflection. And three faces behind you. | The window reflection: shining eyes in the seats behind you |

| 11 | So what are you riding? | Your face |

## C. "Tower 9", Night 1
**Title:** Your Radio Is Calling for Help. In YOUR Voice. 📻
**HUD:** TOWER 9 · NIGHT 1, GENERATOR FUEL meter, chips (03:14 AM / SIGNAL X / TORCHES 2 → 5 → 9 → 23), rule strip.
**Hidden detail:** in the lightning, one of the figures holding a torch wears your orange ranger jacket.

| # | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your radio just crackled. The voice calling for help is yours. | The radio, red light pulsing; hook THE VOICE ON THE RADIO IS YOURS |

| 3 | But you are in tower 9. Alone. Thirty metres above the forest. | Your face under the bulb |
| 4-5 | The last ranger left two rules in the logbook. / Never answer the radio after 3. Never open the hatch. | The logbook page; rule strip |
| 6 | The clock says 3:14. | Wide of the cabin; time card 3:14 AM |
| 7 | The generator has fuel for one more hour. Then the lights go out. | The fuel gauge near E |

| 11 | Then, footsteps on the ladder. Slow. Getting closer. | Down the ladder: a pale hand climbing out of the dark |
| 12 | Three knocks on the hatch. Right under your feet. | The hatch jumps three times; orange light leaks through |
| 13-14 | The radio crackles again. A different voice. / (Base) Tower 9, this is base. Don't open anything. Help is two hours out. | The radio |
| 15 | Two hours. Your fuel has one. | Your face; HELP: 2 HOURS / FUEL: 1 HOUR |
| 16 | Under the hatch, your own voice starts to cry. | The hatch |
| 17 | (Through the hatch, crying) Please. I'm so cold. | You kneel by the hatch |
| 18 | Open it, and you meet whatever has your voice. | The hatch |
| 19 | Keep it shut, and you're in the dark in one hour. | The fuel gauge; the bulb flickers |
| 20 | Whatever most of you choose happens on Night 2. | |
| 21 | So, do you open the hatch for the voice that sounds like you? Or keep it shut, and wait in the dark? | YOUR CALL. OPEN IT / KEEP SHUT |
