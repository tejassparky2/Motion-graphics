Original story. Written against reports/Horror reference study 27 Oct.md and reports/Horror Shorts hooks retention
comments.md. What it takes from the study:
- **Everyday life, at the right time of year.** Trick-or-treating, your little brother, a front door, porch lights.
- **Rules that are clues.** Three rules on a note, each used later as evidence.
- **A test.** "What's my name?"
- **A late flip at ~75%.** The narrator gives the answer ("So the left one is Sam"), then one fact takes it away
  ("Sam can't say the letter R").
- **A clock you can see.** The porch lights go out one by one, and the dark walks toward you.
- **A face in every shot.** A new shot every 2-4 s, with gold-ring close-ups.
- **The vote is a deduction with evidence for both sides.** Left or right; the card stays up 2 s.

**YOU are Riley**, a teenager, walking six kids trick-or-treating on Halloween night. Your little brother Sam, 5, is
the ghost in the bed sheet you cut this morning.
