# Creepy stories, batch 1: scripts for approval (14 Oct 2026)

Inspired by the owner's reference "That's Not My Dad" (2:08, 3D, first person). What makes it work:
- **Something small is wrong** from the first second (he hugs Mom, the kid knows he isn't Dad).
- **Clues pile up**, one per beat, each one a tiny everyday detail (wrong chair, black coffee, the dog afraid).
- **Night confrontation** with one chilling line ("You're next.").
- **Callback ending** to the first clue ("he has always sat in the right chair").

Our versions: **original stories** (not a copy of theirs), told as fiction, with the same engine. Each one is about
45 s (owner's 40-50 s rule), 2D hand-drawn, dark and moody: night palette, lamp and phone glow, flicker, slow push-ins,
shadows under doors, eyes in the dark, a low drone and heartbeat. Turbo voice with tags. Each ends on an either/or
question for the comments (the genie formula).

## A. "Mom Never Calls Me Sweetie" (~42 s)

| # | Narration (voice) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mom calls me down for dinner. "Come eat, sweetie." | Dark upstairs hallway, warm kitchen glow at the bottom of the stairs. Big hook text: **MOM NEVER CALLS ME SWEETIE** |
| 2 | But Mom never calls me sweetie. | Kid frozen at the top step, eyes wide; slow push-in |
| 3 | The kitchen light is on. And someone down there is humming her song. | Long shadow on the kitchen wall swaying; music notes drifting up the stairs |
| 4 | Then my phone buzzes. A text from Mom. | Phone glow lights the kid's face |
| 5 | "Stuck in traffic. Home in an hour." | Phone screen close-up, text bubble types out |
| 6 | [gasp] The humming stops. | Notes vanish; the light flickers; silence (drone drops out) |
| 7 | "Sweetie?" The voice is at the bottom of the stairs now. "Your food is getting cold." | A silhouette at the foot of the stairs, its shadow far too tall |
| 8 | I run to my room. I lock the door. | Fast whip pan; door slams, lock clicks |
| 9 | Then a whisper. From under my bed. | Two eyes blink in the dark under the bed |
| 10 | "Don't open that door. It's me. Mom." | Under-bed eyes; a hand slowly reaches out |
| 11 | My phone buzzes again. "Almost home. Don't let anyone in." | Phone close-up, second text |
| 12 | One voice in the kitchen. One under my bed. One on my phone. | Three cards light up: KITCHEN / BED / PHONE |
| 13 | Only one of them is really Mom. Which one? | The three cards, a "?" over each; comments bait |

Voices: kid narrator (Turbo stock). Kitchen voice and under-bed voice are **the same** warm female voice (so the
viewer can't tell them apart), the kitchen one a touch lower. Tags: [gasp] at 6 only (one tag; silence does the rest).
**Titles:** "Mom Never Calls Me Sweetie... 😨" · "3 Moms. Only 1 Is Real." · "Who's Under My Bed? 🛏️"

## B. "My Twin Came Home Wrong" (~45 s)

| # | Narration (voice) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My twin brother Leo and I get home from summer camp. And Leo hugs Mom with the wrong arm. | Front door, two identical boys with duffel bags; Mom hugging Leo. Hook text: **HE HUGGED HER WITH THE WRONG ARM** |
| 2 | Leo is left-handed. Always has been. | Flash: little Leo drawing with his left hand (old photo look) |
| 3 | This Leo does everything with his right. | Leo now: fork, toothbrush, door handle, all right hand (quick cuts) |
| 4 | At dinner, he eats the mushrooms. Leo hates mushrooms. | Plate close-up; mushroom disappears; Mom smiling, not noticing |
| 5 | Our cat won't go near him. She just hisses from under the table. | Cat's glowing eyes under the table, fur up |
| 6 | That night, I test him. Our secret knock. Two slow. Three fast. | Dark bedroom wall between their rooms; knock rings drawn on the wall: ● ● ●●● |
| 7 | He knocks it back. Perfectly. | Same rings answer from the other side |
| 8 | Then the door creaks open. "Relax, Max. I'm the real Leo." [chuckle] | Leo in the doorway, backlit, smiling a little too long |
| 9 | "But are you the real Max?" | Close-up on Leo's eyes; camera slowly tilts |
| 10 | [gasp] I look down. I'm holding my pencil in my left hand. | Max's hand, pencil in the LEFT hand, heartbeat zoom |
| 11 | I have never been left-handed. | Max staring at his own hand; mirror behind him shows him the "right" way round |
| 12 | Leo is flipped. And so am I. | Split screen: Leo and Max side by side, both mirrored arrows |
| 13 | So who really came home from camp? Leo, me, or neither of us? | Three cards: LEO / MAX / NEITHER, with "?" |

Voices: Max narrates (Turbo stock); Leo is a Turbo character (a younger Kokoro reference, e.g. am_puck). Tags:
[chuckle] at 8, [gasp] at 10.
**Titles:** "My Twin Came Home From Camp... Wrong 😶" · "He Hugged Mom With the Wrong Arm" · "Two Slow. Three Fast. 🚪"

## C. "Rule Number Three" (~46 s) (rules-horror, close to the genie formula: one rule, turned against you)

| # | Narration (voice) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My new night job comes with three rules. By 2 a.m., something asks me to break all of them. | Empty hotel lobby at night, desk lamp, a card in a hand. Hook text: **RULE 3: THE PASSWORD IS BLUEBERRY** |
| 2 | Rule one. Never use the elevator after midnight. | Card line 1 lights up; elevator doors in the dark, floor light flickering |
| 3 | Rule two. Room 4 is always empty. | Card line 2; a hallway, door "4" with a dusty "Do not disturb" |
| 4 | Rule three. If I ever call you, I'll say the password. Blueberry. | Card line 3; the boss (friendly, mustache) winking, a blueberry icon |
| 5 | At 2 a.m., the desk phone rings. The screen says: room 4. | Clock flips to 2:00; phone screen "ROOM 4", red light blinking |
| 6 | A woman's voice. "Could you bring up some towels?" | Phone receiver close-up; a faint figure reflected in the lobby window |
| 7 | [gasp] I hang up. It rings again. | Receiver slammed; phone rings again, shaking |
| 8 | It's my boss. "Hey. The lady in room 4 needs towels. Take the elevator. It's faster." | Boss voice; elevator light pings on by itself |
| 9 | "Blueberry." | Big blueberry stamp: PASSWORD ✓ |
| 10 | The password is right. | Card rule 3 glows green |
| 11 | But the call is coming from room 4. | Phone screen zoom: "ROOM 4"; rule 2 glows red, rule 1 glows red |
| 12 | So. Do you take the towels up? Or do you follow the rules? | Two cards: TAKE THE TOWELS / FOLLOW THE RULES |

Voices: guard narrates (Turbo stock); the woman (Turbo character, af_heart reference); the boss (Turbo character,
am_michael reference). Tags: [gasp] at 7.
**Titles:** "Rule 3: The Password Is Blueberry 🫐" · "Never Answer Room 4" · "Night Guard Rules: Would You Go Up?"
